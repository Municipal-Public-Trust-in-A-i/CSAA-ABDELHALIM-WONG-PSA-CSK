(c) All documents that were filed, lodged, or admitted into evidence in the underlying action, including pleadings, discovery responses, deposition transcripts, exhibits, motions, declarations, and court orders.

I will forward copies of each of these documents to your office for review and confirmation.

This stipulation would be limited to authenticity only -- that is, that each document is a true and correct copy of what it purports to be and was filed with or maintained by the Court. It would not constitute an admission by CSAA as to the truth of any factual assertions contained within those documents.

These are public court records subject to judicial notice under Evidence Code sections 452(c) and 452(d), and certified copies are self-authenticating under Evidence Code sections 1530 and 1532. A stipulation avoids unnecessary authentication motions and serves the interests of both sides.
