# Filing Hierarchy — What Plaintiff Can File Based on Defense Pleadings In Place

**Jurisdiction:** California (CCP) + CRC + SF Local Rules
**Cases:** 801 (CGC-25-631801) and 802 (CGC-25-631802)
**Current defense posture:** Anti-SLAPP motions (CCP §425.16) AND Demurrers (CCP §430.10) pending on both cases; Plaintiff has motion for leave to file SAC pending.

---

## 1. WHEN AN ANTI-SLAPP (CCP §425.16) IS ON FILE

### 1.1 Automatic discovery stay — CCP §425.16(g)
- **1.1.1** All discovery proceedings in the action are **STAYED** upon filing of the anti-SLAPP motion.
- **1.1.2** Stay remains until the court has ruled on the motion.
- **1.1.3** Exception: on noticed motion and for good cause shown, court may order specified discovery.
- **1.1.4** "Discovery" = interrogatories, RFPs, RFAs, depositions, subpoenas (including third-party).

### 1.2 What Plaintiff MAY still file while anti-SLAPP is pending
- **1.2.1** Opposition to the anti-SLAPP itself (due 9 court days before hearing — CCP §1005(b)).
- **1.2.2** Supporting declarations and admissible evidence (§425.16(b)(2)).
- **1.2.3** Request for specified discovery under §425.16(g) — noticed motion, good cause.
- **1.2.4** Motion to strike portions of moving party's evidence (evidentiary objections).
- **1.2.5** Request for judicial notice (Evid. Code §§451–452).
- **1.2.6** Opposition to fee request (if brought concurrently).
- **1.2.7** Ex parte applications to preserve evidence / for TRO (non-discovery relief).
- **1.2.8** Notice of related cases / CMC statements (case-management is not "discovery").

### 1.3 What Plaintiff CANNOT file / serve while anti-SLAPP is pending
- **1.3.1** Any discovery request (written or deposition notices).
- **1.3.2** Motion to compel discovery (no underlying discovery allowed).
- **1.3.3** Amended complaint as of right once anti-SLAPP is filed — **§425.16 bars amendment to defeat the motion** (*Simmons v. Allstate* (2001) 92 Cal.App.4th 1068; *Salma v. Capon* (2008) 161 Cal.App.4th 1275).
  - **1.3.3.1** Amendment after anti-SLAPP service requires leave AND is generally disfavored if used to avoid ruling.
  - **1.3.3.2** Pre-service amendment (before anti-SLAPP is filed) does NOT defeat the motion; defendant re-files against amended complaint.

### 1.4 Timing
- **1.4.1** Anti-SLAPP must be filed within **60 days of service of complaint** (court discretion thereafter) — §425.16(f).
- **1.4.2** Hearing within **30 days of service** unless docket requires later — §425.16(f).
- **1.4.3** Opposition: **9 court days** before hearing; Reply: **5 court days** before — CCP §1005(b).
- **1.4.4** Denial is **immediately appealable** → automatic stay of further trial-court proceedings pending appeal (CCP §425.16(i), §904.1(a)(13)).

## 2. WHEN A DEMURRER (CCP §430.10) IS ON FILE

### 2.1 No automatic discovery stay
- **2.1.1** Demurrer alone does **NOT** stay discovery.
- **2.1.2** BUT if anti-SLAPP is also pending → §425.16(g) stay controls (see §1.1).

### 2.2 What Plaintiff MAY file while demurrer is pending
- **2.2.1** Opposition (9 court days before hearing — CCP §1005(b)).
- **2.2.2** Request for judicial notice.
- **2.2.3** Motion for leave to amend (CCP §473(a)) — **preferred path**; amendment is liberally granted.
- **2.2.4** First Amended Complaint **as of right** if no responsive pleading has been served (CCP §472(a)) — one time before opposition to demurrer is due, OR after meet-and-confer per §430.41.
- **2.2.5** SAC / further amendment by noticed motion under §473(a) + §576.
- **2.2.6** Discovery (subject to §1.1 if anti-SLAPP also pending).
- **2.2.7** CMC statements, notices of related case, ex parte for scheduling.
- **2.2.8** Motion for preliminary injunction / TRO (substantive relief).

### 2.3 CCP §472(a) — amendment as of right
- **2.3.1** Plaintiff may amend ONCE without leave any time before **answer, demurrer, or motion to strike is filed**, OR after such motion is filed but before opposition to it is due.
- **2.3.2** Must comply with **§430.41 meet-and-confer** 5 days before filing.
- **2.3.3** Amendment typically moots the pending demurrer.
- **2.3.4** ⚠️ If anti-SLAPP is also pending, §472(a) amendment is limited — cannot be used to defeat the anti-SLAPP (see §1.3.3).

### 2.4 Timing
- **2.4.1** Demurrer: opposition 9 court days, reply 5 court days before hearing (§1005(b)).
- **2.4.2** Hearing must be within **30 days after filing** unless court orders otherwise — CCP §430.40(a) for defendant; court schedules.
- **2.4.3** If sustained with leave: 10 days to amend (unless otherwise ordered) — CCP §472.

## 3. COMBINED POSTURE — ANTI-SLAPP + DEMURRER BOTH PENDING (your current state on 801 and 802)

### 3.1 Controlling rule: anti-SLAPP stay dominates
- **3.1.1** Discovery stay under §425.16(g) applies to the ENTIRE action, not just claims challenged by the anti-SLAPP.
- **3.1.2** Plaintiff cannot cure via §472(a) amendment to escape either motion if used as end-run around anti-SLAPP.

### 3.2 Hearing sequence
- **3.2.1** Court typically hears **anti-SLAPP FIRST** (it is dispositive and appealable).
- **3.2.2** Demurrer may be **held in abeyance** or heard same day, but ruling on demurrer deferred until anti-SLAPP resolved.
- **3.2.3** If anti-SLAPP granted → stricken claims dismissed; remaining claims proceed to demurrer.
- **3.2.4** If anti-SLAPP denied → defendant's 60-day appeal clock runs; trial court action stays pending appeal (§425.16(i)).

### 3.3 What Plaintiff CAN file right now (801 & 802)
- **3.3.1** Opposition to anti-SLAPP + supporting declarations + evidentiary objections.
- **3.3.2** Opposition to demurrer.
- **3.3.3** **Motion for leave to file SAC (CCP §473(a))** — ALREADY pending; must argue amendment is not solely to defeat anti-SLAPP; new causes of action must have independent factual/legal support.
- **3.3.4** Reply ISO SAC motion (9/5-day rule).
- **3.3.5** Noticed motion for §425.16(g) specified discovery (good cause).
- **3.3.6** Request for judicial notice / bench briefs / lodgings.
- **3.3.7** Proposed orders (CRC 3.1312).
- **3.3.8** Ex parte for scheduling / shortening time / staying briefing.
- **3.3.9** POS-050 service paperwork.
- **3.3.10** Notice of ruling (after hearing — CRC 3.1312).

### 3.4 What Plaintiff CANNOT / SHOULD NOT file
- **3.4.1** Any discovery demand (written discovery, depo notices, subpoenas) — barred by §425.16(g).
- **3.4.2** Motion to compel (no underlying discovery permitted).
- **3.4.3** FAC/SAC filed as of right under §472(a) as a shortcut to moot the anti-SLAPP — will be struck or ignored; *Simmons* / *Salma*.
- **3.4.4** Motion for summary judgment/adjudication (premature — discovery stayed, pleadings unsettled).
- **3.4.5** Trial-setting conference requests (case is not at-issue).

## 4. SPECIAL FILING CATEGORIES (always permitted regardless of posture)

### 4.1 Case-management / housekeeping
- **4.1.1** CMC statements (CRC 3.722).
- **4.1.2** Notice of related case (CRC 3.300).
- **4.1.3** Substitution of attorney / notice of limited scope.
- **4.1.4** Change of address; association of counsel.
- **4.1.5** Notice of lodging; notice of errata.
- **4.1.6** Proofs of service (POS-010/POS-050).

### 4.2 Jurisdictional / constitutional
- **4.2.1** Opposition to motion to quash service.
- **4.2.2** Opposition to forum non conveniens / stay.
- **4.2.3** Constitutional challenges / due-process objections.

### 4.3 Protective / emergency
- **4.3.1** TRO / preliminary injunction (CCP §527) — substantive, not discovery.
- **4.3.2** Writ petitions (extraordinary review).
- **4.3.3** Motion to seal / protective order (CRC 2.550–2.551).
- **4.3.4** Ex parte to preserve evidence (spoliation risk).

### 4.4 Sanctions / evidentiary
- **4.4.1** Motion for CCP §128.7 sanctions (21-day safe harbor).
- **4.4.2** Evidentiary objections to declarations.
- **4.4.3** Motion to strike improper pleadings (§435–437).

## 6. DECISION TREE — "CAN I FILE THIS TODAY?"

```
Is it discovery (RFP/ROG/RFA/depo/subpoena)?
├── YES → Anti-SLAPP pending? 
│         ├── YES → NO, unless §425.16(g) noticed motion granted
│         └── NO  → YES
└── NO  → Is it an amendment (FAC/SAC)?
          ├── YES → Anti-SLAPP pending?
          │         ├── YES → Only via noticed §473(a) motion; must show independent purpose
          │         └── NO  → §472(a) as-of-right OR §473(a) noticed motion
          └── NO  → Is it opposition / RJN / declaration / proposed order / POS?
                    ├── YES → ALWAYS permitted; mind 9/5-day clock
                    └── NO  → Is it substantive relief (TRO/PI/writ/seal)?
                              ├── YES → Permitted (not barred by §425.16(g))
                              └── NO  → Case management — always permitted
```

## 7. KEY AUTHORITIES

- **CCP §425.16** — anti-SLAPP; (g) discovery stay; (i) appeal
- **CCP §430.10, §430.41, §430.40** — demurrer grounds, meet-and-confer, timing
- **CCP §472(a), §473(a), §576** — amendment as of right / by leave
- **CCP §1005(b)** — 16/9/5 day motion calendar
- **CCP §1010.6** — electronic service
- **CRC 3.1110, 3.1112, 3.1113, 3.1300, 3.1312** — motion format, memos, ex parte, proposed orders
- *Simmons v. Allstate Ins. Co.* (2001) 92 Cal.App.4th 1068 — no amend-out of anti-SLAPP
- *Salma v. Capon* (2008) 161 Cal.App.4th 1275 — same
- *Varian Medical Systems v. Delfino* (2005) 35 Cal.4th 180 — automatic stay on anti-SLAPP appeal
- *Schaffer v. City and County of SF* (2008) 168 Cal.App.4th 992 — §425.16(g) scope
