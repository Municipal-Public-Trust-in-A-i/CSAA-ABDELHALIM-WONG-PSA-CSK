# Comprehensive Discovery Analysis & Logical Path Mapping: Case No. 631802

## I. Overview of the Case and Claims
**Causes of Action:** 
1. Fraud and Deceit (Civ. Code §§ 1709-1710)
2. Violation of Business & Professions Code § 17200 (UCL)
**Defendant:** CSAA Insurance Exchange.
**Core Theory:** CSAA engaged in institutional bad faith by issuing a denial letter on February 25, 2021, falsely representing that its investigation was "concluded" and its insured was "not liable." In reality, CSAA had not obtained or reviewed the 911 audio or body-worn camera footage, which contained the insured's admission of fault. This violates California Insurance Code § 790.03 and triggers UCL/Fraud liability.

## II. Strategic Logical Mapping
The master strategy for Case 631802 is designed to weaponize CSAA's own claims manual and the California Fair Claims Settlement Practices Regulations against them. If CSAA tries to blame trial counsel (PSA) for the courtroom fraud, these RFAs ensure CSAA remains on the hook for the *original* bad faith denial in 2021 that necessitated the lawsuit in the first place.
