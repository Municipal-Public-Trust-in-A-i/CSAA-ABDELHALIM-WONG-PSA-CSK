# Comprehensive Discovery Analysis & Logical Path Mapping: Case No. 631801

## I. Overview of the Case and Claims
**Cause of Action:** Independent Action in Equity to Set Aside Judgment for Extrinsic Fraud.
**Defendants:** Subhi Abdelhalim, CSAA, Phillips, Spallas & Angstadt LLP (PSA), Priya Navaratnasingham, Alberto Reyna, Michael Chambers, Carbone Smith & Koyama.
**Core Theory:** Defense counsel, with the knowledge and funding of CSAA, possessed key discovery materials (911 audio, body-worn camera footage, CAD logs) for over 20 months, yet falsely represented to the Court that these materials "were not produced in discovery" and were of "unknown provenance" or "news to me." This prevented Plaintiff from fully presenting his case, constituting extrinsic fraud.

## II. Strategic Logical Mapping
The master strategy for Case 631801 uses RFAs to lock down the chronological timeline (Produced Aug 2022 -> Transferred May 2023 -> Hidden Jan/Feb 2025 -> Pivot April 2025). This creates an inescapable trap: 
- **If they deny the timeline**, documentary evidence (transcripts, emails) destroys their credibility and yields cost-of-proof sanctions.
- **If they admit the timeline**, they confess to Extrinsic Fraud.
