| Authority | Holding (one line) | Relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Nguyen-Lam v. Cao* (2009) 171 Cal.App.4th 858, 871–872 | Noticed leave to amend under §§ 473(a)(1) / 576 may be heard after a § 425.16 motion is filed, where the plaintiff has fully opposed the anti-SLAPP. | Defeats any categorical “cannot move for leave to amend while anti-SLAPP is pending” rule. |
| *JKC3H8 v. Colton* (2013) 221 Cal.App.4th 468 | CCP § 425.16(f)’s 60-day “renewed special motion” rule is the statutory place for the consequences of a superseding amended pleading. | The Legislature set the sequencing remedy; amendment does not void anti-SLAPP policy. |
| *Foreman & Clark Corp. v. Fallon* (1971) 3 Cal.3d 875, 884 | An amended pleading that supersedes a prior version moots motions that target only the superseded pleading. | If leave is granted and SAC is filed, FAC-only motions are governed by *Foreman & Clark*. |
