## Recurring defense themes (both MPAs)

| Theme | Defense conclusory (paraphrase) | Counter-hook (from cross-case memos) |
|--------|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Recoverability** | FAC “improperly” seeks damages “not recoverable as a matter of law” / “unsupported by any statutory, contractual, or factual basis” | Tie **damages** to **elements** of **fraud**, **UCL**, **equity**; use **Day-7 admission**, **denial letter** vs **actual knowledge** (spine facts) |
| **Verdict / 9–3** | Underlying PI case **defense verdict**; relitigation / “collateral attack” | **Extrinsic** / **fraud on court** is **not** the same “issue” as **negligence**; **appeal** (A173827) goes to **instructional** / **record** errors, not **duplicative** money |
| **§ 47(b) / privilege** | Conduct “absolutely privileged” as part of **judicial proceeding** | **Ribas** exception / **fraud** channel; **fraud on court** theories; **completed investigation** vs **no knowledge** pair in 802 narrative |
| **CCP § 436** | “Irrelevant, false, or improper” / “cannot be cured by amendment” | **Discretionary** strike; **factual** fraud allegations may survive **facial** attack or require **evidence** — frame for **denial** or **narrowing** |
| **Estoppel / Singh line** | “Collateral estoppel precludes relitigation …” | **Issue** vs **claim** ID; **void** judgment **void** attack; **extrinsic** **fraud** **standard** |
