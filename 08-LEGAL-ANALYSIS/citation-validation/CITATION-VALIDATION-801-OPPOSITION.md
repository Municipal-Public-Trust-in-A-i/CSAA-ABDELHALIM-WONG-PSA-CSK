## Mis-characterization flags (for Reply argument)

- **Colton v. Rupert Murdoch** (206 Cal.App.4th 722 / 772): defense omits the anti-subversion qualifier -- Colton bars amendment **to avoid** a pending anti-SLAPP, not concurrent 473(a)(1)/576 motions that confront the anti-SLAPP on the merits and add non-communicative-conduct theories (the Park/Kimmel gravamen filter).
- **Contreras v. Dowling**: confined to facts where the plaintiff amended **after** an adverse ruling on the same protected activity and with no new operative conduct. It does not announce a blanket freeze; the Fourth District in *Schoendorf* (and Supreme Court in *Baral*) confirm that § 425.16 operates claim-by-claim and allows amendments that cleanly identify non-protected conduct.
- **JKC3H8 v. Colton**: **NOT cited in 801 Opposition** -- Reply will not rebut it in 801 (only 802).
- **Notice (9 days)**: defense asks Court to deny solely because original notice was 9 days. With the hearing reset to May 6, 2026, plaintiff's notice is now 18+ days -- full CCP 1005(b) compliance. Defense concedes no prejudice; alternatively, OST/ex parte stay application pending in Dept 301 renders the objection moot.
- **CCP § 425.16(f)**: defense argues forfeiture if SAC is permitted. The statute expressly preserves a **60-day renewed-motion window**; plaintiff does not seek to strip that right.
- **Discovery stay (§ 425.16(g))**: defense treats as a blanket freeze on all amendments; the statute is a narrow gatekeeping rule against discovery **on the protected claim**, not on unrelated amendment motions.
