Code of Civil Procedure § 391, declaring a litigant vexatious requires
meeting strict, unambiguous statutory criteria:

   preceding seven years that were finally determined adversely. (you've had
   one case against each of those defendants, not five.)

   propria persona, the validity of a final determination, cause of action, or

   unnecessary delay in ongoing litigation. (the exact opposite is true you're
   the one who created the delays at Porter Scott, you're the defense that's
