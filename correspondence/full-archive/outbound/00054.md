Just to remind you about how the law works I think maybe this LAW SCHOOL
101  review might help.
*1. The Demurrer: The "So What?" Motion*

*The Concept:* A Demurrer says: *"Even if everything the plaintiff says is
100% true, there is no legal 'case' here."* The judge only looks at the
piece of paper (the Complaint), not outside evidence.
<#the-three-common-defense-%22fumbles%22%3A>*The Three Common Defense
"Fumbles":*

   1. *The "Appeal Masquerade" (Wrong Role):* * *The Error:* The defense
   tells the judge, *"You shouldn't hear this case because the plaintiff is
   just trying to appeal a different judge's decision."* * *Legal 101:* A
   trial judge isn't an appellate judge. However, if the plaintiff is suing
   for a *new* reason (like fraud that happened behind the scenes), it’s a
   new case, not an appeal. The defense is trying to trick the judge into
   acting like a higher court.
   2. *The "Label-as-Fact" Error:* * *The Error:* The defense says, *"This
   lawsuit is a 'Collateral Attack' (an illegal attempt to redo a case)."*
    * *Legal 101:* In a Demurrer, the judge must accept the *Plaintiff's
   facts* as true. The defense's "labels" (calling it a collateral attack
   or a scam) are *arguments*, not facts. The defense is trying to sneak
   their opinions in as if they were proven truths.
   3. *The "Merits" Trap:* * *The Error:* The defense tries to argue that
   the plaintiff will lose at trial because the evidence is weak.
      - *Legal 101:* You don't look at evidence in a Demurrer! You only
      look at the "pleading" (the story). Arguing about who will win
the "merits"
      of the case at this stage is like trying to give a book review after only
      reading the front cover.

------------------------------
<#2.-the-anti-slapp%3A-the-%22free-speech%22-shield>*2. The Anti-SLAPP: The
"Free Speech" Shield*

*The Concept:* This motion is meant to stop "Strategic Lawsuits Against
Public Participation." It protects people from being sued for speaking out
or petitioning the government. It has two steps:

   - *Step 1:* Did the defendant do something involving free
   speech/petitioning?
   - *Step 2:* Does the plaintiff have a "minimal merit" case?

<#the-three-common-defense-%22fumbles%22%3A-1>*The Three Common Defense
"Fumbles":*

   1. *"Step-Two Creep":* * *The Error:* The defense starts arguing that
   the plaintiff has no evidence (Step 2) before they have even proven that
   the case involves free speech (Step 1).
      - *Legal 101:* You can't skip Step 1. If the lawsuit is about a "bad
      act" (like a business contract violation) and not "speech," the
Anti-SLAPP
      shield doesn't even apply.
   2. *The "Everything is Petitioning" Fallacy:* * *The Error:* The defense
   argues, *"We were in a courtroom when this happened, so everything we
   did is protected speech."*
      - *Legal 101:* Just because you are in a courthouse doesn't mean
      everything you do is protected. If you punch someone in a
courthouse, it’s
      still battery. The law (the *Park* case) says the judge must look at
      the *specific act* that caused the injury, not just the "litigation
      environment."
   3. *The "Privilege Blob":* * *The Error:* The defense uses the
   "Litigation Privilege" (a law that says you can't be sued for what you say
   in court) as a "Universal Solvent" to wash away the whole case.
      - *Legal 101:* The Litigation Privilege is a defense you use at
      trial; the Anti-SLAPP is a motion to strike. The defense often blurs them
      together to make it look like the case is impossible to win,
even when the
      conduct (like extrinsic fraud) isn't covered by that privilege.

------------------------------
<#summary-table-for-the-class>*Summary Table for the Class*
Defense Tactic The "Legal 101" Correction
*"This is just an illegal appeal!"* If the plaintiff alleges *new* fraud or
rights, it's a new lawsuit, not an appeal.
*"The Plaintiff's case is a 'scam'."* That’s an opinion. At Demurrer, we
only look at the Plaintiff's alleged facts.
*"We were in court, so we are immune."* Being in court isn't a "get out of
jail free" card for non-speech injuries (*Park* rule).
*"The Plaintiff has no evidence!"* Too early! We don't weigh evidence
during the first step of an Anti-SLAPP.
