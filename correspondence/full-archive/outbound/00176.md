PLAINTIFF'S POSITION

Defendant has claimed that a stay of all proceedings, including discovery,
flows from the filing of its anti-SLAPP motion. Plaintiff respectfully
disagrees and has documented this position in the authority notices
identified below. The governing authority establishes:

   - CCP §425.17(c) exempts from anti-SLAPP protection any defendant that
   is an insurer providing commercial insurance services to the public,
   including CSAA Insurance Exchange;
   - *Flatley v. Mauro* (2006) 39 Cal.4th 299 holds that anti-SLAPP
   protections cannot be invoked where the underlying conduct is illegal as a
   matter of law;
   - *JKC3H8 v. Colton* and *Newport Harbor* confirm the threshold
   commercial-speech exemption and pleading management standards applicable to
   this motion;
   - CCP §425.16(g) expressly authorizes the Court to permit specified
   discovery upon noticed motion, notwithstanding any stay;
   - The May 12, 2026 minute order in the consolidated case CGC-25-631801,
   and the April 30, 2026 minute order denying the MFL-SAC in this case, are
   before the Court as lodged judicial notice items.

Plaintiff does not concede the anti-SLAPP stay is valid, does not concede
discovery is suspended, and has filed a formal motion under CCP §425.16(g)
for specified discovery as a protective measure in the event the Court
declines to find the exemption dispositive at the June 24 hearing.
