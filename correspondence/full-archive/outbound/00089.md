> The correct five California Supreme Court authorities on amendment to add
> a cause of action are:
>
>    1. Klopstock v. Superior Court (1941) 17 Cal.2d 13 — the foundational
>    "liberal policy" holding and the express permission to add new causes of
>    action by amendment.
>    2. Desny v. Wilder (1956) 46 Cal.2d 715 — Supreme Court holding that
>    leave to amend to state additional theories/causes of action must be
>    granted unless the plaintiff is unable to plead them.
>    3. Trafton v. Youngblood (1968) 69 Cal.2d 17 — Supreme Court holding
>    that an amended pleading is permitted even to allege a new cause of action,
>    absent prejudice.
>    4. Mesler v. Bragg Management Co. (1985) 39 Cal.3d 290 — Supreme Court
>    reaffirmation that amendment to add a cause of action (there, alter-ego) is
>    proper even at trial absent prejudice.
>    5. Foreman & Clark Corp. v. Fallon (1971) 3 Cal.3d 875 — Supreme Court
>    holding that the amended pleading supersedes the original (the procedural
>    consequence that makes amendment effective).
