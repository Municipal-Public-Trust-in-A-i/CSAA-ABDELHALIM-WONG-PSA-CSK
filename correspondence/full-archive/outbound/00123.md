*1. Threshold off-ramps under CCP § 425.17 (sequential, decided before §
425.16).*

Section 425.17 is structurally prior to § 425.16. The Court does not reach
prong-one gravamen until § 425.17 is cleared. The 802 record presents both
subdivisions of the exemption squarely:

   -

   *CCP § **425.17(c)*
   <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=425.17.>*
   — commercial-speech exemption.* A 21-day "concluded investigation"
   denial letter from a commercial insurer (CSAA) to an unrepresented direct
   third-party claimant, on the carrier's own claim form (Claim No.
   1004-09-1054), is commercial communication about the carrier's own business
   operations. Authority: *Simpson Strong-Tie Co. v. Gore*
   <https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/2010/s164174.html>
   (2010) 49 Cal.4th 12, 30–32.
   -

   *CCP § **425.17(b)*
   <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=425.17.>*
   — public-interest exemption.* The 802 action seeks broad-based,
   non-individualized relief tied to insurer claim-handling practices that
   affect the public, satisfying the *Club Members for an Honest Election
   v. Sierra Club*
   <https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/4th/45/309.html>
   (2008) 45 Cal.4th 309, 316–318 framework.
   -

   *CCP § **425.17(e)*
   <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=425.17.>*
   — exemption order is non-appealable.* A § 425.17 ruling in Plaintiff's
   favor is *not* within the § 425.16(i) interlocutory appeal pathway and
   does *not* trigger the § 425.16(g) automatic stay. CCP § 904.1(a)(13)
   <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=904.1.>
   is read in conjunction.

*4. Litigation privilege misfit (Civ. Code § 47(b)).*

The litigation privilege protects communicative acts. Civ. Code § 47(b)
<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=47.>;
*Kimmel v. Goland*
<https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/3d/51/202.html>
(1990) 51 Cal.3d 202, 211; *Rusheen*, supra. Premature claim-file closure
(802) and a May 4, 2023 file-transfer omission (801) are non-communicative
acts; institutional "deny-delay-defend" practice (TAC ¶¶ 28–30, 802) is
non-communicative. The privilege does not stretch backward to immunize the
underlying acts merely because a later denial letter was written *about*
them.
