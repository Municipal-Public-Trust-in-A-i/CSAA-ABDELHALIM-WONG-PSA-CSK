# Reusable face banner: reservation of rights

> **Reservation of rights.** This archive is published as speech and as a public record of correspondence already sent or received. Completeness is the point. Completeness is not a grant of immunity.
>
> **1. Speech.** This archive is protected expression under the **First Amendment** to the United States Constitution and **article I, section 2** of the California Constitution. It is a public record of correspondence already sent or already received. Posting it here does not convert it into a pleading.
>
> **2. Petition.** Formal positions remain solely those stated in papers already on file. The right to petition the government for redress is reserved under **article I, section 3** of the California Constitution. This archive is not a substitute petition and is not a new filing.
>
> **3. Due process / retaliation.** Exercise of speech and petition rights cannot be used to deny **Fourteenth Amendment** due process or **article I, section 7** of the California Constitution, or to punish access to the courts. Code of Civil Procedure section 391 is a statutory finding. It is not a speech code.
>
> **4. What it is not.** This is **not** a court paper. It is not a pleading, motion, memorandum, declaration, proof of service, request for judicial notice, Evidence Code section 1152 settlement offer, or Code of Civil Procedure section 128.7 safe-harbor notice. It is not new service. It does not restart any 128.7 clock. It is **not** First Amendment immunity from CCP 391. Completeness is not a grant of immunity.
>
> Publishing this archive is not service. This page is not a filing.
