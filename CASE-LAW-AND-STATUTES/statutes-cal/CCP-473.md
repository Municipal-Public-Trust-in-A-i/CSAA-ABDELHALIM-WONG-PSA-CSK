# Code of Civil Procedure section 473

**Citation:** CCP § 473 | **Source:** California Legislative Information (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov)

---

## Statutory Text (subd. (a)(1), amendment in furtherance of justice)

**Note:** Section 473 has multiple subdivisions and has been amended over time. Confirm current text on leginfo before filing.

Subdivision (a)(1) (traditional "liberal amendment" provision) includes, in substance, that **the court may, on terms that may be just, allow a party to amend any pleading or proceeding** by adding or striking the name of a party, or by correcting a mistake in the name of a party or a mistake in any other respect, **and may, upon like terms, enlarge the time for answer or demurrer**.

The statute further provides that **the court may likewise, in its discretion, and upon terms as may be just, allow the pleadings or proceedings to be amended, or a party to be added, by substituting the true name for a fictitious name**, or by adding other parties, **at any stage of the proceedings**.
