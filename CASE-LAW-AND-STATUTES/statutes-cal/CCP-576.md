# Code of Civil Procedure section 576

**Citation:** CCP § 576 | **Source:** California Legislative Information (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov)

---

## Statutory Text (excerpt)

**Note:** Confirm current text and subdivision numbering on leginfo before filing.

Section 576 addresses **orders after demurrer** and, in relevant part, the court's authority **to allow amendments** when a demurrer is sustained (including authority to allow a reasonable time to amend in specified circumstances).
