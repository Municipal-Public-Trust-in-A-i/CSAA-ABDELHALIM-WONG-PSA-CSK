# California Rules of Court, Rule 8.204 (Contents and format of briefs)

**Citation:** Cal. Rules of Court, rule 8.204 | **Source:** courts.ca.gov

---

## Key Provisions

**(a) Contents**
- Table of contents and table of authorities (cases, constitutions, statutes, court rules, other authorities)
- Each point under separate heading with argument and citations
- Record references with volume and page number
- Appellant's opening brief: nature of action, relief sought, judgment/order appealed from; finality; summary of significant facts

**(b) Format (paper)**
- 8½ x 11 inch paper, white or unbleached, at least 20-pound weight
- Font: roman style, at least 13-point; case names italicized or underscored
- Line spacing: at least 1.5; headings/footnotes may be single-spaced
- Margins: at least 1½ inches left/right, 1 inch top/bottom
- Pages numbered consecutively (cover = page 1), Arabic numerals

**(c) Length**
- Computer-produced: max 14,000 words including footnotes; certificate of word count required
- Typewriter: max 50 pages
- Petition for rehearing: max 7,000 words or 25 pages

---

**Relevance in case:** Appellate brief format compliance (font, spacing, margins); CRC 8.74 applies to electronic documents.
