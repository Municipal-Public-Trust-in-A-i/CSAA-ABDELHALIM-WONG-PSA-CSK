# California Rules of Court, Rule 8.74 (Electronic format)

**Citation:** Cal. Rules of Court, rule 8.74 | **Source:** courts.ca.gov

---

## Key Provisions

Rule 8.74 governs electronic filing format requirements for appellate documents, including PDF format, bookmarks, and compliance with court rules for documents submitted in electronic form. Applies in conjunction with CRC 8.204 (brief format) for electronic submissions.
