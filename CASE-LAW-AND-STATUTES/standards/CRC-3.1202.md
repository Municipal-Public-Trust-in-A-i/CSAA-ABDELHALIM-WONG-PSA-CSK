# California Rules of Court, Rule 3.1202 (Ex parte applications)

**Citation:** Cal. Rules of Court, rule 3.1202(c) | **Source:** courts.ca.gov

---

## Summary

Rule 3.1202 governs ex parte applications. Subdivision (c) addresses showing of irreparable harm and other requirements for ex parte relief.

**Relevance in case:** Ex parte relief; irreparable harm.
