# California Rules of Court, rule 3.1324

**Citation:** Cal. Rules of Court, rule 3.1324 | **Source:** courts.ca.gov / California Rules of Court (confirm current rule text before filing)

---

## Purpose

Rule **3.1324** sets requirements for **motions to amend pleadings** in unlimited civil cases, including that the motion be supported by a **declaration** stating:

- the **effect** of the amendment;
- why the amendment is **necessary and proper**;
- when the facts giving rise to the amended allegations were **discovered**; and
- why the request was **not made earlier**.

The rule also requires attaching a **copy of the proposed amended pleading**, appropriately marked.
