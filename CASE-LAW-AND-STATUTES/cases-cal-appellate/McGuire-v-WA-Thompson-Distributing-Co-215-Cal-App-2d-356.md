# McGuire v. W.A. Thompson Distributing Co.

**Citation:** 215 Cal.App.2d 356 (1963)

**Court:** California Court of Appeal

**Date:** 1963

---

## Facts / Holding

During deliberations, the court properly used a "not in evidence" rationale for municipal-court/deposition material that "would not be evidence" unless "read ... during the trial." Demonstrates that "not in evidence" is a testimony/exhibit gatekeeping concept (what was read/admitted at trial), not a legal standard for withholding governing statutory law. CCP section 614 separately covers when jurors "desire to be informed of any point of law."
