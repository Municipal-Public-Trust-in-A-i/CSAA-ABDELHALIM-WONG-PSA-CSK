# Aldrich v. San Fernando Valley Lumber Co.

**Citation:** 170 Cal.App.3d 725 (1985)

**Court:** California Court of Appeal, Second District, Division Three

**Date:** July 29, 1985

**Docket:** B007068

---

## Facts / Holding

Plaintiff Aldrich, a dentist, was injured in an automobile accident. His action was dismissed for failure to answer interrogatories. The trial court later granted his motion to vacate the dismissal. The Court of Appeal affirmed the order vacating the dismissal. Cited for extrinsic fraud: false statements inducing procedural/evidentiary rulings.
