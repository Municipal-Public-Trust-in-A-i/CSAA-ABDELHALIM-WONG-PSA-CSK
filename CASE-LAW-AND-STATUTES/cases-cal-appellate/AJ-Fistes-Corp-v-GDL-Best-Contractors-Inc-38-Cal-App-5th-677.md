# A.J. Fistes Corp. v. GDL Best Contractors, Inc.

**Citation:** 38 Cal.App.5th 677 (2019)

**Court:** California Court of Appeal, Second District, Division Seven

**Date:** Filed July 16, 2019; modified and certified for publication August 13, 2019

**Docket:** B283662

**Trial Court:** Los Angeles County Superior Court, No. BS161779

**Source PDF:** `cases-cal-appellate/2019-b283662.pdf`

---

## Why This Case Matters

This opinion is useful for two recurring demurrer issues:

1. **Taxpayer standing under Code Civ. Proc. section 526a.** A corporation that paid state taxes funding a local agency can have standing to challenge illegal expenditures by that agency.

2. **Uncertainty demurrers.** The court reiterates the high bar for uncertainty and explains that pleading defects under California Rules of Court, rule 2.112 generally warrant leave to amend, not dismissal without leave.

---

## Facts / Holding

Plaintiff Fistes alleged a school district unlawfully awarded a public remediation contract to another bidder and paid public funds under an invalid contract. The trial court sustained a demurrer without leave to amend, finding no taxpayer standing and fatal uncertainty.

The Court of Appeal reversed. It held:

- The 2018 amendment to Code Civ. Proc. section 526a applied to the pending appeal.
- Allegations that the plaintiff paid state taxes funding the school district were sufficient to plead taxpayer standing.
- The third amended complaint was not so incomprehensible as to justify an uncertainty demurrer.
- Any rule 2.112 pleading defect should have been handled by granting leave to amend.
- Alter ego and restitution allegations against the individual corporate officers were insufficiently pleaded, but leave to amend should still have been granted.

---

## Key Holdings

### 1. Section 526a standing extends to taxes funding the agency

The amendment to section 526a applies where the plaintiff corporation paid "a tax that funds the defendant local agency." The court rejected the argument that the tax must fund the specific challenged project rather than the agency itself.

**Use for:** standing to challenge unlawful expenditures by school districts or other local agencies when the plaintiff paid state-level taxes that fund the agency.

### 2. Amendment expanding standing can apply during a pending appeal

The court treated the statutory change as prospective because it changed who may sue, not the defendants' substantive liability for past conduct.

**Use for:** arguing a new standing statute or amendment may be invoked on remand or appeal when it does not alter substantive liabilities.

### 3. Uncertainty demurrers are strongly disfavored

The court repeats the familiar rule that a demurrer for uncertainty is proper only where the pleading is so incomprehensible that the defendant cannot reasonably respond.

**Use for:** opposing boilerplate uncertainty attacks on detailed, intelligible pleadings.

### 4. Rule 2.112 defects usually call for amendment, not dismissal

Even where a complaint does not neatly label or separately state causes of action, the trial court must look to substance over form. If the pleading's factual allegations apprise the defendant of the issues, uncertainty should be overruled or leave to amend granted.

**Use for:** rebutting arguments that imperfect pleading labels justify dismissal without leave.

### 5. Ownership/control allegations alone do not establish alter ego

The court held that allegations the individual defendants controlled the corporation and benefited from its receipts were insufficient, without more, to establish unity of interest and inequitable result.

**Use for:** alter ego pleading standards and leave-to-amend analysis.

## Searchable Quotations

### Uncertainty

> "`[D]emurrers for uncertainty are disfavored, and are granted only if the pleading is so incomprehensible that a defendant cannot reasonably respond.`"

> "`A demurrer for uncertainty is strictly construed, even where a complaint is in some respects uncertain, because ambiguities can be clarified under modern discovery procedures.`"

> "`[U]nder our liberal pleading rules, where the complaint contains substantive factual allegations sufficiently apprising defendant of the issues it is being asked to meet, a demurrer for uncertainty should be overruled or plaintiff given leave to amend.`"

> "To the extent the third amended complaint violated California Rules of Court, rule 2.112, by failing to explain the nature of the alleged cause of action, the trial court should have granted leave to amend to correct this deficiency."

> "Rule 2.112 does not require the plaintiff to separate its claims into separate causes of action."

### Taxpayer Standing

> "Section 526a, subdivision (a), provides a corporation that has been assessed and is liable to pay or has paid within one year before the commencement of an action 'a tax that funds the defendant local agency' has standing to sue to restrain an illegal expenditure of public funds by the local agency."

> "The plain language of the amendment requires only that the tax fund the agency, not the challenged agency action."

> "By alleging payment of state taxes, Fistes has adequately alleged payment of 'a tax that funds' the District."

### Leave To Amend

> "We cannot say there is no 'reasonable possibility that the defect can be cured by amendment.' On remand, the court should grant Fistes an opportunity to amend ..."

## Practical Relevance To Current Demurrer Work

For demurrer opposition purposes, the most useful point is that `Fistes` does **not** support routine uncertainty attacks against an organized pleading. It treats uncertainty as an extreme remedy reserved for pleadings so incomprehensible that a defendant cannot frame a response, and it favors leave to amend over dismissal when the complaint's substance is understandable.
