# In re Marriage of Baltins

**Citation:** 212 Cal.App.3d 66, 260 Cal.Rptr. 403 (1989)

**Court:** California Court of Appeal, First District, Division Three

**Date:** July 18, 1989

---

## Facts / Holding

Marriage dissolution proceeding. Husband appealed an order granting wife's motion to set aside the property and support provisions of the dissolution judgment on grounds of duress and extrinsic fraud or mistake. The court addressed the modern definition of duress, which is less stringent than the definition codified in Civil Code section 1569. Court affirmed both the order setting aside the property settlement and a subsequent order granting the wife's motion for modification of support. Cited for extrinsic fraud; procedural rulings induced by misrepresentation.
