# Silberg v. Anderson

**Citation:** 50 Cal.3d 205 (1990)

**Court:** Supreme Court of California

**Date:** February 26, 1990

**Docket:** S007056

---

## Facts / Holding

Physician Barry Neil Silberg sued Margaret Anderson, his former wife's divorce attorney, for damages. During divorce proceedings, Silberg proposed retaining a psychologist for custody evaluations; Anderson recommended a psychologist but allegedly failed to disclose she was romantically involved with that psychologist. Silberg claimed this resulted in a biased report that damaged his reputation and prevented visitation.

**Key holding:** The litigation privilege under Civil Code section 47(2) is absolute. The Supreme Court unanimously affirmed the absolute nature of the privilege, rejecting any "interest of justice" exception. Even potentially unethical attorney conduct during litigation is protected by the privilege; such conduct may be addressed through bar discipline or criminal charges. Cited for litigation privilege four-part test.
