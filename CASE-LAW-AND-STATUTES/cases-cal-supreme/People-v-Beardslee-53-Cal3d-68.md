# People v. Beardslee

**Citation:** 53 Cal.3d 68, 279 Cal.Rptr. 276, 806 P.2d 1311 (1991)

**Court:** Supreme Court of California

**Date:** 1991

---

## Facts / Holding

Donald Jay Beardslee was convicted of two counts of first-degree murder with special circumstances for the 1981 killings of Patty Geddling and Stacey Benjamin in Redwood City. He was sentenced to death. Cited for court duty to answer jury questions.
