# Prentice v. North American Title Guar. Corp.

**Citation:** 59 Cal.2d 618 (1963)

**Court:** Supreme Court of California

**Date:** 1963

---

## Facts / Holding

Establishes the "tort-of-another" or "Prentice" doctrine. A party forced into litigation with a third party by the tortious conduct of another may recover attorney fees and litigation costs as consequential damages. The doctrine applies where the defendant's tort "involves" the plaintiff in litigation with a third party.
