# Rowland v. Christian

**Citation:** 69 Cal.2d 108 (1968)

**Court:** Supreme Court of California

**Date:** August 8, 1968

---

## Facts / Holding

Plaintiff James David Rowland, Jr., was a guest in defendant Nancy Christian's apartment when he was injured using a bathroom faucet. The faucet handle broke, causing severed tendons and nerve damage. Christian had known the handle was cracked but did not warn Rowland.

**Key holding:** Eliminated the traditional common law distinctions between invitees, licensees, and trespassers. The court held that an occupant of property owes a general duty of care to any visitor, even a trespasser, replacing the previous framework where different duties applied depending on visitor status.
