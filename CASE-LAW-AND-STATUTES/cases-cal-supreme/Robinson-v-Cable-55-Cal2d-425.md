# Robinson v. Cable

**Citation:** 55 Cal.2d 425 (1961)

**Court:** Supreme Court of California

**Date:** March 2, 1961

---

## Facts / Holding

Personal injury action arising from a motor vehicle collision. Plaintiffs were guests in a station wagon that collided with defendant Cable's gasoline tank-truck on a two-lane highway. The trial court instructed the jury that if plaintiffs could have observed and warned the driver of the truck's approach but failed to do so, they would be barred from recovery.

**Key holding:** The Supreme Court found this instruction erroneous. It imposed an improper burden of care on automobile passengers. Absent specific facts warranting heightened attention, passengers have no duty to observe traffic and warn drivers of approaching vehicles. Case remanded for retrial.
