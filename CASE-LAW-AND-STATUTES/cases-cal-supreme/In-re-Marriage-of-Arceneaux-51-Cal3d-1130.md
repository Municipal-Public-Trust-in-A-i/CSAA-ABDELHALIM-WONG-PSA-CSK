# In re Marriage of Arceneaux

**Citation:** 51 Cal.3d 1130, 275 Cal.Rptr. 797, 800 P.2d 1227 (1990)

**Court:** Supreme Court of California

**Date:** December 13, 1990

---

## Facts / Holding

Marriage dissolution action. The trial court orally announced its decision; husband requested a statement of decision, which wife prepared and the court signed. Husband did not object to the proposed or final statement at trial. On appeal, he claimed the statement was deficient in multiple respects.

**Key holding:** A litigant waives the right to appeal alleged deficiencies in a trial court's statement of decision by failing to raise them before the trial court (CCP section 634). Appellate courts may make implied findings in favor of the prevailing party. Judgments are presumed correct on appeal; litigants must object at trial to preserve appellate issues.
