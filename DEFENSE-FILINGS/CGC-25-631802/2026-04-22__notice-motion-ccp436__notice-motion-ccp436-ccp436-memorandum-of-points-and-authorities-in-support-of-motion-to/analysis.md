## Summary of Systemic Deficiencies

1. **Selective citation pattern.** The MPA consistently omits controlling or directly applicable authority while citing general propositions. The American Rule (CCP 1021) is cited without its three recognized exceptions; Bus. & Prof. Code section 17203 is cited for standing limitations while its affirmative grant of injunctive and restitutionary authority is ignored.

2. **Conclusory characterizations.** Multiple categories (lost earnings, future damages, punitive damages) are dismissed as "speculative" or lacking "basis" without engaging with the specific factual allegations in the FAC or the applicable pleading-stage standards.

3. **Mischaracterization of cause-of-action scope.** The MPA treats the UCL cause of action as if it were the only claim, ignoring that emotional distress, punitive damages, and consequential damages are tied to the tort and Insurance Code causes of action in the FAC.

4. **Failure to address the eggshell-plaintiff doctrine.** The FAC specifically alleges defendant's knowledge of Plaintiff's vulnerabilities. The MPA's remoteness/causation arguments for health harm and emotional distress fail to address this established doctrine.

5. **No engagement with Insurance Code violations.** The MPA does not address how Insurance Code violations (unfair claims settlement practices) independently support multiple damages categories, including punitive damages and attorney fees.
