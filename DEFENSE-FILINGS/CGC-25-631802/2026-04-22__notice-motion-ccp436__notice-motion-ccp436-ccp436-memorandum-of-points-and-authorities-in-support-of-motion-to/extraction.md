## Category Summaries

### (a) Reliance / Economic Injury

Defense argues that Plaintiff cannot recover reliance-based economic injury under the UCL because the FAC (paragraphs 23-24) fails to allege the requisite "lost money or property" traceable to the unfair practice. Cites Bus. & Prof. Code section 17200 as limiting recoverable damages to restitution, not affirmative damages.

### (b) Attorney-Fee Damages

Defense contends that third-party litigation fees incurred by Plaintiff (FAC paragraphs 26-27) are not recoverable as damages. Argues these are consequential costs of separate litigation, not damages flowing from the insurer's conduct, and that no statutory or contractual fee-shifting provision applies.

### (c) Lost Earnings, Lost Productive Time

Defense characterizes lost earnings and lost productive time (FAC paragraphs 30-32) as speculative and unrecoverable. Argues that Plaintiff has not established a causal chain between the insurer's conduct and any earnings loss, and that the damages are inherently conjectural.

### (d) Health-Related Harm, Exacerbation

Defense argues health-related harms alleged in FAC paragraphs 33-36 lack causation and are too remote from the insurance dispute. Contends that the insurer's claims-handling conduct is not the proximate cause of physical health deterioration and that such damages are not cognizable in an insurance coverage dispute.

### (e) Emotional Distress, Humiliation

Defense takes the position that emotional distress damages (FAC paragraphs 37-40) are not recoverable in a UCL action or an insurance coverage dispute. Argues that the causes of action do not support standalone emotional distress recovery.

### (f) Medical/Transportation/Household Assistance

Defense argues special damages for medical expenses, transportation costs, and household assistance are not properly pleaded. Contends that the FAC fails to meet the specificity requirements for special damages under CCP 425.11 and related authority.

### Punitive Damages

Defense seeks to strike punitive damages allegations (FAC paragraphs 1, 45, 47, 61(5)), arguing no basis exists for punitive damages against an insurer. References Civ. Code section 3294 and contends the FAC does not plead facts supporting oppression, fraud, or malice with the required specificity.

### Attorney Fees

Defense argues there is no fee-shifting basis for Plaintiff's attorney fee claim (FAC paragraph 28). Cites CCP section 1021 (the American Rule) and contends no statutory exception applies.

### Future Damages

Defense characterizes future damages claims as speculative and not recoverable. Argues insufficient factual basis to support an award of prospective damages of any category.

### Injunctive Relief

Defense argues Plaintiff lacks standing for injunctive relief. Cites Bus. & Prof. Code section 17203 and contends Plaintiff has not demonstrated the likelihood of future harm necessary to support injunctive standing.

### Prejudgment Interest

Defense argues prejudgment interest is not available on unliquidated tort claims. Cites Civ. Code section 3287 and contends the damages at issue are not liquidated or ascertainable by calculation, precluding interest from a date certain.
