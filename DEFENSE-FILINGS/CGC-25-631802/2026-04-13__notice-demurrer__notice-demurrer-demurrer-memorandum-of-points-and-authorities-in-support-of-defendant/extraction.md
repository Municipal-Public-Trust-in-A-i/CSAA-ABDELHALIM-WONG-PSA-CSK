## Summary of Each Heading

### V.A.1 -- No Jurisdiction (Appeal Pending)

Defense argues that the pending appeal of the judgment in the prior action (CGC-21-594102) under CCP 904.1(a)(13) deprives the Superior Court of jurisdiction over this action. The argument treats the appeal as a blanket jurisdictional bar.

### V.A.2 -- Another Action Pending

Defense invokes CCP 430.10(c) as a plea in abatement, contending that the existence of CGC-21-594102 (or its appeal) constitutes "another action pending" between the same parties on the same cause of action.

### V.A.3.i -- Fraud Insufficient

Defense contends the fraud cause of action fails to meet the heightened pleading standard under CCP 430.10(e). The argument asserts that the FAC does not plead fraud with the required specificity (who, what, when, where, how).

### V.A.3.ii -- UCL Cannot Survive Moradi-Shalal

Defense argues that because Moradi-Shalal v. Fireman's Fund (1988) 46 Cal.3d 287 eliminated private rights of action under Insurance Code 790.03(h), a UCL claim predicated on violations of that section cannot stand. The argument treats Moradi-Shalal as a complete bar to UCL predicate liability.

### V.A.4 -- Uncertainty

Defense argues the FAC is uncertain under CCP 430.10(f), claiming the pleading is ambiguous and unintelligible as to the causes of action asserted.

### V.B -- Civil Code 47(b) Protects Denial

Defense invokes the litigation privilege under Civil Code 47(b), citing Silberg v. Anderson (1990) 50 Cal.3d 205, to argue that the claim denial and related communications are privileged and therefore cannot support any cause of action.

### V.C -- Collateral Estoppel

Defense argues collateral estoppel bars relitigation of issues already determined in CGC-21-594102. The argument asserts identity of issues between the prior action and this one.

### V.D -- Statute of Limitations

Defense contends all claims are time-barred, identifying the accrual date as February 25, 2021 (the date of the claim denial). Under CCP 338 (three-year) and CCP 339 (two-year) limitations periods, defense argues all claims expired before the FAC was filed.

### V.F -- No Standing

Defense argues plaintiff lacks standing to bring UCL claims predicated on Insurance Code 790.03(h), relying on Moradi-Shalal and citing Norton, Omega Video, and Shaolian as supporting authority. Defense also invokes the standing requirements of Business and Professions Code 17204.
