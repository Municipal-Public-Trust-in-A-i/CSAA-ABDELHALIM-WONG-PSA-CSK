14

AND MOOTED BY THE COURT’S JULY 15 ORDER

15

Section 1008(a)’s ten-day clock is tied to written notice of entry of the order. It is a timing rule,

16

not a license to treat a Court-continued calendar as void. (Le Francois v. Goel (2005) 35 Cal.4th

17

1094, 1107-1108 [court’s inherent authority to reconsider prior rulings].)

18

The Defense OPP freezes the record “as of June 24, 2026,” asserts that Defendants’ proposed order

19

remains pending signature, and concludes the motion “is not yet ripe.” (RJN Item 3.) However,

20

this Court already managed that timing point on July 15, 2026, by continuing the motion to July

21

31, 2026, before the same judge, specifically to accommodate order-entry posture. (RJN Item 1.)

22

Once a signed order and notice of entry exist, July 31 is the merits calendar the Court set. A

23

procedural dismissal labeled as “ripeness” would improperly override the Court’s own July 15

24

continuance order.
