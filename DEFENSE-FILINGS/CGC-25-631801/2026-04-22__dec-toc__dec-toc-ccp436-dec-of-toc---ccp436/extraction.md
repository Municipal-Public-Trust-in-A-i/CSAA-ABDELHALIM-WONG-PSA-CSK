Nature of Independent Action: An independent action in equity is an ancient remedy, preserved

Definition of Extrinsic Fraud: Extrinsic fraud occurs when a party is deprived of the opportunity

4

5

issues. It encompasses conduct where a party is kept in ignorance of the suit, or, as in this case,

6

7

the judicial system effectively. (Kulchar, supra, 1 Cal.3d at p. 471; Aldrich v. San Fernando Valley

8

Lumber Co. (1985) 170 Cal.App.3d 725, 735.)

12

from legally taking p~ace on the merits. It includes fraud that corrupts the judicial process itself,

14

based on materially false information.
