## One-sentence core

Plaintiff is pressing a **consistent record** on two timelines at once: **(i)** the **Apr. 30, 2026** hearing on **leave to file a further amended complaint** (SAC), and **(ii)** the **May 13, 2026** calendar for the **demurrer and special motion to strike**—while using **lodgment** and **discovery** instruments to lock in what was already served and to push defaults where the defense has stalled.

## What each lane does

### A. SAC / Apr. 30 lane (`mfl-sac-apr30-hearing/`)

Purpose: give the court a **single, clerk-friendly** stack for the **leave-to-amend** setting—bench brief, **adverse authority**, **safe-harbor** supplement, **meet-and-confer** paper trail, **proofs of service**, and the **defense opposition** as exhibit context.  

Strategy: frame the hearing as **procedural regularity** (timing, prejudice, relation to the Anti-SLAPP calendar) plus **substantive continuity** with the **Moradi-Shalal** common-law carve-out analysis already in the multi-case record.

### B. Motion-to-strike response (`tac-response-motion-strike/`)

Purpose: complete the **TAC** cure package—**memorandum, declaration, RFJN, proposed order, Third Amended Complaint exhibit, cure matrix**—so the “motion to strike” posture cannot be argued as “no operative pleading.”  

Strategy: every **demurrer / strike** argument is answered **on the face of the TAC** and in a **side-by-side cure matrix** map.

### C. Anti-SLAPP lodgment (`antislap-tac-notice-of-lodging/` + `prong-one-notice-of-lodging/`)

Purpose: **notice of lodgment** routes exist so the court (and the public record) can see **what was already lodged** for **prong analysis** and for the **proposed TAC** as an exhibit.  

Strategy: separate **prong-one** lodgment from the **TAC-focused** lodgment so departments and research audiences can find the right spine without opening a single mega-PDF.

### D. Discovery pressure (`discovery-track1-*` through `track3`)

Purpose:

- **Track 1** — convert silence on prior discovery into a **deemed / compel / sanctions** motion record with a **separate statement** and supporting exhibits.  
- **Track 2** — open a **fresh sixth set** of written discovery to **current** fact disputes.  
- **Track 3** — **notice of default** and **CMC statement** update so the **case-management** record reflects non-compliance and the need for teeth.

Strategy: **parallel tracks** avoid a single point of failure; defaults and motion practice reinforce that the case is **not** frozen behind strike/demurrer briefing.

### E. Defense service master (`defense-service-docuserve/`)

Purpose: one **chronological / omnibus** PDF for **proof-of-service** completeness across the burst, plus a **standalone POS** for lean service.  

Strategy: clerks and e-filing vendors get **one** drop for “everything served,” without replacing the **topic-sorted** folders above.
