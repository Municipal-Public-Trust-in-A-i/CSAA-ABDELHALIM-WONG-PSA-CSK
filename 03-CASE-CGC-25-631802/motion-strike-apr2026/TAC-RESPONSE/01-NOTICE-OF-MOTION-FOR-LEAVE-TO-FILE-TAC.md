## GROUNDS FOR MOTION

This Motion is made on the following grounds:

### 1. Liberal Amendment Policy

California courts have a strong policy favoring resolution of disputes on their merits. Code of Civil Procedure § 473 grants courts broad discretion to allow amendments to pleadings, and that discretion should be exercised liberally in favor of amendments. *Nestle v. City of Santa Monica* (1972) 6 Cal.3d 920, 939.

### 2. Response to Defense CCP § 436 Motion — Specific Cure of Seven Objections

The proposed Third Amended Complaint specifically cures the seven objections raised in Defendant's April 22, 2026 Motion to Strike under CCP § 436:

| Defense Objection | TAC Cure Section |
|-------------------|------------------|
| (1) *Trope* — pro-per fees | Section III: Deletion; *Prentice* damages element |
| (2) Oppression / malice | Section IV: *Lazar* particulars; deny-delay-defend pattern |
| (3) Fraud particularity | Section IV: Sarah Rash; Claim No. 1004-09-1054; Feb. 25, 2021 |
| (4) Litigation privilege / *Ribas* | Section II: Pre-litigation carveout; non-communicative conduct; *Ribas* distinction |
| (5) Standing / *Shaolian* | Section I.5: Direct-addressee theory |
| (6) Collateral estoppel / § 1032 | Section VI: Primary rights; express PI disclaimer |
| (7) Investigation standard | Section VII: Title 10 CCR § 2695.7 |

### 3. No Prejudice to Defendant

Defendant will not be prejudiced by the proposed amendments:

- The TAC does not add any new parties.
- The TAC does not assert fundamentally new theories of liability—fraud, UCL, and bad faith were already alleged. The TAC pleads these theories with greater specificity.
- No additional discovery should be necessary.
- The case is at an early stage.

### 4. Good Faith Amendment

The proposed amendments are made in good faith to address objections raised in Defendant's CCP § 436 Motion to Strike. Plaintiff has diligently responded to the motion by preparing curative amendments.

### 5. Meritorious Claims Supported by Record

The proposed Third Amended Complaint states meritorious claims supported by the certified record, including:

- The February 25, 2021 denial letter (Claim No. 1004-09-1054, signed by Sarah Rash);
- The three-anchor delayed-discovery sequence (Jan. 17, 2025 / Feb. 18, 2025 / Apr. 16, 2025);
- The "deny-delay-defend" institutional pattern documented by the underlying record.
