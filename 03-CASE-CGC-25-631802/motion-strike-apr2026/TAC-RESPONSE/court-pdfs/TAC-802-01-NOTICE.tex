\section{GROUNDS FOR MOTION}\label{grounds-for-motion}

This Motion is made on the following grounds:

\subsection{1. Liberal Amendment Policy}\label{liberal-amendment-policy}

California courts have a strong policy favoring resolution of disputes on their merits. Code of Civil Procedure \textsection{} 473 grants courts broad discretion to allow amendments to pleadings, and that discretion should be exercised liberally in favor of amendments. \emph{Nestle v. City of Santa Monica} (1972) 6 Cal.3d 920, 939.

\subsection{3. No Prejudice to Defendant}\label{no-prejudice-to-defendant}

Defendant will not be prejudiced by the proposed amendments:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  The TAC does not add any new parties.
\item
  The TAC does not assert fundamentally new theories of liability---fraud, UCL, and bad faith were already alleged. The TAC pleads these theories with greater specificity.
\item
  No additional discovery should be necessary.
\item
  The case is at an early stage.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{4. Good Faith Amendment}\label{good-faith-amendment}

The proposed amendments are made in good faith to address objections raised in Defendant's CCP \textsection{} 436 Motion to Strike. Plaintiff has diligently responded to the motion by preparing curative amendments.
