**Two-Layer Cure Strategy:** The pending SAC-leave motion (hearing Apr 30, 2026) is the **first** cure. This TAC-RESPONSE packet is the **second** cure, filed only if/when the SAC is granted and Round D defects surface in subsequent defense motions.

This packet responds to Defendant CSAA's Motion to Strike filed April 22, 2026 under CCP § 436. The TAC implements all curative amendments to:

1. **Cure Shaolian objection** — Direct-Duty gravamen with Title 10 CCR §§ 2695.4, 2695.7
2. **Cure § 47(b) privilege** — Pre-Litigation Privilege Carveout for denial-letter conduct
3. **Cure Trope objection** — Attorney's fees cure (delete pro-per fees, add Prentice damages)
4. **Cure Lazar particularity** — Punitive damages specificity with § 3294 elements
5. **Cure damages vagueness** — Compensatory/injunctive damages rewrite with causation
6. **Cure duplicative relief** — Primary Rights/Reservation paragraph
