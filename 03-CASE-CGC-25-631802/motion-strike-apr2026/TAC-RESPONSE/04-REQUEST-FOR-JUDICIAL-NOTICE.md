## IV. LEGAL BASIS

### A. Court Records Are Proper Subjects of Judicial Notice

Evidence Code § 452(d) permits judicial notice of court records. The documents listed as Exhibits A through H are records of the Superior Court of California, County of San Francisco.

### B. Facts Not Reasonably Subject to Dispute

Evidence Code § 452(h) permits judicial notice of facts capable of immediate and accurate determination by resort to sources of reasonably indisputable accuracy. The CSAA denial letter (Exhibit I) is a document whose existence and contents are not reasonably subject to dispute.

### C. Statutes and Regulations

Evidence Code §§ 452(a) and (b) permit judicial notice of statutes and regulations. The statutes and regulations listed as Exhibits J through P are proper subjects of mandatory judicial notice.

### D. Judicial Notice Is Mandatory Upon Proper Request

Evidence Code § 453 provides that the trial court shall take judicial notice of any matter specified in Section 452 if a party requests it and gives adverse parties sufficient notice.
