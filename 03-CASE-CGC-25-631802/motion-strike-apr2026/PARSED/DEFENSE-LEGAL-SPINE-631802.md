## 5A. Defense authority errors (seven clusters)

The 802 MPA (MPA2-CCP436) carries seven discrete clusters of authority error. For the full breakdown with MPA2 line citations and dispositive uncited authorities, see [SIDE-BY-SIDE/DEFENSE-THEORY-ANALYSIS-802-ONLY.md](../../SIDE-BY-SIDE/DEFENSE-THEORY-ANALYSIS-802-ONLY.md).

- **A. Litigation Privilege Overreach (Civ. Code § 47(b) / *Ribas*).** Ignores *Action Apartment* 41 Cal.4th 1232, 1248–49 (pre-litigation timing), *Flatley v. Mauro* 39 Cal.4th 299, 317–21 (illegality carve-out), and *Rusheen v. Cohen* 37 Cal.4th 1048, 1058 (non-communicative conduct). TAC cures at ¶¶ 14–21, 16A, 21A.
- **B. Standing Overreach (*Shaolian* / *Norton* / *Omega*).** Ignores Civ. Code § 2338 principal-agent liability; *Doctors' Company* 49 Cal.3d 39; *Moradi-Shalal* carve-out under *State Farm* 45 Cal.App.4th 1093. TAC cures at ¶¶ 5–6, 6A.
- **C. Collateral-Estoppel / Primary-Rights Misuse.** Cites *F.E.V.* / *Singh* / *Wouldridge* / *Throckmorton* without walking through *Lucido* 51 Cal.3d 335 factors or *Mycogen* 28 Cal.4th 888 / *Crowley* 8 Cal.4th 666 primary-rights. TAC cures at ¶¶ 41–44, 41A, 44A.
- **D. *Trope* / Pro-Per-Fees Overreach.** Ignores *Prentice* 59 Cal.2d 618 tort-measure damages exception. TAC cures at ¶¶ 22–24, 24A (including $75k cost-judgment re-framing).
- **E. *Lazar* / Punitive-Pleading Conflation.** Conflates trial-evidentiary standard (*Angelia P.*) with pleading standard; ignores *G.D. Searle* 49 Cal.App.3d 22, 29. TAC cures at ¶¶ 25–30, 25A.
- **F. § 436 Used as a Demurrer Substitute.** Sufficiency arguments belong in demurrer (§ 430.10(e)/(f)); § 436 is narrow. TAC ¶ 52A separately narrows anti-SLAPP overlap.
- **G. Self-Defeating 2021/2025 Contradiction.** "Concluded investigation" (2021) vs. "news to me" (2025) cannot both be true; October 29, 2024 Day-7 "provided by Dolan" transcript resolves against the defense. TAC ¶¶ 6A and 47A put the lever in issue.
