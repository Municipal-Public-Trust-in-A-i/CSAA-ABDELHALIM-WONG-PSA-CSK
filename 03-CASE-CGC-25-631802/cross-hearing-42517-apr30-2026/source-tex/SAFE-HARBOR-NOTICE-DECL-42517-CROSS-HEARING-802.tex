By this Notice Plaintiff triggers that harbor concerning the challenged matters described herein. Plaintiff \textbf{reserves all rights}; nothing herein waives any motion, objection, citation to authority, sanctions request in other vehicles, regulatory complaint, fee motion, opposition, amendment, judicial notice objection, remedy, appeal, extraordinary writ petition, tolling or discovery argument expressly or impliedly waived.
