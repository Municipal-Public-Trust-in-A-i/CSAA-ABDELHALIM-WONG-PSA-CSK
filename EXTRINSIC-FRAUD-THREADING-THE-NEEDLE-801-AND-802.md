# Threading the Needle: Why CGC-25-631801 Is Extrinsic Fraud (Not Intrinsic), and How the Same Theory Maps Onto CGC-25-631802

**Cases:** CGC-25-631801 (Equity / Vacatur) and CGC-25-631802 (Fraud / UCL against CSAA)
**Underlying action:** CGC-21-594102
**Purpose:** Single integrated doctrinal explanation linking the two extrinsic-fraud theories at their separate layers and showing the carrier-funded ratification chain that connects them.

---

## Part I — Why CGC-25-631801 Pleads Extrinsic, Not Intrinsic, Fraud

The line between intrinsic and extrinsic fraud is not "how bad was the lie." It is **where the lie landed and what it corrupted.** Apply the test from [*Kulchar v. Kulchar* (1969) 1 Cal.3d 467](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/opinions-pdf/Demurrer-cal-3d-vol1-p467-Kulchar-v-Kulchar.pdf) ([narrative](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/cases-cal-supreme/Kulchar-v-Kulchar-1-Cal3d-467.md)) [Leg.Auth. #1](#leg-auth-1) and *Caldwell v. Taylor* (1933) 218 Cal. 471, 476 ([gap / external](LEGAL-AUTHORITIES.md#gaps-and-external-fallbacks)) [Leg.Auth. #2](#leg-auth-2), and the 801 facts fall on the extrinsic side of the line on five independent axes simultaneously. ([Legal authorities](LEGAL-AUTHORITIES.md).)

### 1. The Kulchar test: target of the deception

> **[4] Extrinsic fraud usually arises when a party is denied a fair adversary hearing because he has been "deliberately kept in ignorance of the action or proceeding, or in some other way fraudulently prevented from presenting his claim or defense."**
> — [*Kulchar v. Kulchar* (1969) 1 Cal.3d 467, 471](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/cases-cal-supreme/Kulchar-v-Kulchar-1-Cal3d-467.md) · [opinion PDF](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/opinions-pdf/Demurrer-cal-3d-vol1-p467-Kulchar-v-Kulchar.pdf)

- **Intrinsic fraud** = a lie aimed at the **jury / fact-finder on the merits** (perjured testimony about who ran the red light). The adversary system is presumed adequate; cross-examination is supposed to catch it.
- **Extrinsic fraud** = a lie aimed at the **tribunal itself** that distorts the *frame* of the contest (concealing the action, blocking presentation, manipulating procedural rulings).

In 801, the false statements were not made to the jury about a fact at issue (liability, causation, damages). They were made **to Judge Wong, in chambers and in motions in limine**, about the **discovery, custody, and provenance** of evidence. That is a structural-procedural lie, not a merits lie.

### 2. The Caldwell "regularity vs. merits" rule

> **"Where the fraud goes to the regularity of the proceedings, rather than to the merits, and prevents the aggrieved party from having a fair hearing, the fraud is extrinsic."**
> — *Caldwell v. Taylor* (1933) 218 Cal. 471, 476

The Navaratnasingham misrepresentations went to the **regularity of the proceedings:**

- whether to grant a continuance,
- whether to grant MIL No. 6 / MIL No. 17,
- whether to admit the supplemental police report,
- whether to admit the 911 in plaintiff's case-in-chief or defer it.

None of those rulings were merits calls the jury weighed. They were **procedural gates** the judge controlled, and each gate was set on a false factual premise.

### 3. The deception was directed at the judge, not the jury (*fraus illata curiae*)

| Doctrine | Operation |
|---|---|
| **Intrinsic fraud** | A witness lies on the stand about a fact (e.g., the color of a traffic light). The adversary system is expected to expose the lie via cross-examination. The system works as designed. |
| **Extrinsic fraud** | Opposing counsel lies **directly to the judge** about possessing the body-cam footage of the traffic light, causing the judge to exclude it from trial entirely. The latter is an existential threat to the adversary system. |

The January 17, 2025 MIL declarations ("no idea where this came from"), the February 18, 2025 in-court statements ("news to me"; "did not receive until the MSC"), and the April 16, 2025 "not received as part of the subpoena" line were all on-the-record-to-the-bench representations made by an officer of the court — **not testimony the jury weighed.** Cross-examination cannot reach a Bus. & Prof. Code § 6068(d) artifice made in MIL papers. [Leg.Auth. #17](#leg-auth-17)

### 4. The harm was "prevented from presenting," not "lost on the merits"

This is the doctrinally cleanest cut. *Kulchar*'s extrinsic-fraud trigger is being **"fraudulently prevented from presenting [your] claim."** The 801 prejudice catalog maps onto that verb exactly:

| Ruling procured by the misrepresentation | What plaintiff was *prevented* from doing |
|---|---|
| Continuance denied (Hr'g Tr. p. 181) | Preparing — depositions of BWC witnesses, authentication, coherent trial plan |
| MIL No. 17 granted (subject to discovery) | Starting trial with 911/CAD admissible; forced to relitigate from an excluded posture |
| Supplemental police report excluded (RT 7, ll. 2888–2896) | **Permanently** — never seen by the jury; would have corroborated plaintiff's account |
| 911 deferred until after plaintiff testified (RT 7, ll. 3024–3057) | Presenting Abdelhalim's "BOOM! I just hit him" admission *in case-in-chief* in proper sequence |

None of these is "the jury heard a lie." Each is "the judge — relying on a lie — built a procedural cage around plaintiff's case." That is the textbook *Kulchar* "prevented from presenting" injury.

### 5. *Pico v. Cohn* / "should have been guarded against at trial" — why the safety valve does not close

The classic objection to upgrading something to extrinsic is `Pico v. Cohn` (1891) 91 Cal. 129: if the litigant could have caught it at trial with diligence, it stays intrinsic. That defense fails here because:

- The truth was held by the **defense alone** — the May 4, 2023 substitution gave PSA twenty months of exclusive custody of the Carbone / Chambers file. Plaintiff *could not* have known what was in the defense file.
- Plaintiff is *pro per*. The misrepresentations were made in MIL papers and bench colloquies where the ability to cross-examine the *attorney* is structurally absent.
- The artifice was a **"subpoena shield"** — answering possession questions with statements about subpoena returns. That cannot be impeached in real time without the very documents being suppressed.

### 6. The Navaratnasingham "Dolan admission" is what *converts* this from intrinsic to extrinsic

The April 16, 2025 trial transcript captures the conversion event:

> **MS. NAVARATNASINGHAM:** "Sure. They were provided by Dolan. He has had them for whatever, sure."
> — RT 7, p. 214, ll. 13–15

That single line:

- Destroys the defense's good-faith defense by proving specific knowledge of the chain of custody;
- Triggers judicial estoppel against every prior representation of non-receipt and unknown provenance;
- Violates Cal. R. Prof. Conduct 3.3 and [Bus. & Prof. Code § 6068(d)](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/statutes-cal/BPC-6068.md) by exposing the artifice;
- **Elevates the conduct from intrinsic to extrinsic fraud** by showing that the Court was deceived into making rulings that prevented plaintiff from fully presenting his case.

Without this admission, the defense could still characterize the January 17 / February 18 statements as good-faith mistakes about a fact the jury ultimately got to weigh — i.e., intrinsic. With the admission, scienter is locked, the target is the bench, and the harm is procedural exclusion. All three Kulchar factors are satisfied **on the four corners of the certified Reporter's Transcript.**

### The needle, threaded in one paragraph

801 is extrinsic, not intrinsic, because the lies were not told to the jury about a disputed merits fact (which the adversary system is designed to police via cross-examination), but to the **trial judge** about the **discovery provenance and custody** of evidence — and the judge **relied** on those lies to make **procedural** rulings (continuance denial, MIL No. 17 grant, supplemental-report exclusion, 911 deferral) that **prevented plaintiff from "fully exhibiting his case"** within the *Kulchar* meaning. The April 16, 2025 "provided by Dolan" admission is the in-record confession that converts what could have been characterized as intrinsic perjury into a *Caldwell* "regularity of the proceedings" fraud — corruption of the **frame** of the trial, not the **merits** within the frame. That is the entire reason the equity action (CGC-25-631801) lies in the first place: appellate review can only test rulings against the record the trial judge saw, but only an equity court with original-jurisdiction fact-finding power can pierce the record to reach the lie *behind* the record. ([*Kulchar v. Kulchar* (1969) 1 Cal.3d 467, 471](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/cases-cal-supreme/Kulchar-v-Kulchar-1-Cal3d-467.md); *Caldwell v. Taylor* (1933) 218 Cal. 471, 476 — [external](LEGAL-AUTHORITIES.md#gaps-and-external-fallbacks); [*Aldrich v. San Fernando Valley Lumber Co.* (1985) 170 Cal.App.3d 725, 735](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/cases-cal-appellate/Aldrich-v-San-Fernando-Valley-Lumber-Co-170-Cal-App-3d-725.md) · [PDF](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/opinions-pdf/Demurrer-cal-app-3d-vol170-p725-Aldrich-v-San-Fernando-Valley-Lumber-Co.pdf).) ## Part II — How the Same Theory Maps Onto CGC-25-631802

The 802 fraud is not a clone of the 801 fraud. It lives at a different layer of the same continuous deception:

- **801** alleges extrinsic fraud at the **trial-court layer** (in-litigation suppression by counsel).
- **802** alleges extrinsic fraud at the **pre-litigation claims-handling layer** (institutional concealment by CSAA via the February 25, 2021 denial letter), then uses the *ratification* and *Prentice* doctrines to attach the 801 trial-fraud back onto the carrier as proximate, foreseeable consequence.

The doctrinal architecture is set out in `DEFENSE/802/12-LEGAL-AUTHORITY-ROADMAP-AND-SAC-MAP.md` §§ E-5, E-12, and the proposed SAC ¶¶ 20–23, 22A, 22B, 35, 35A, 48B–C, 53.

### Axis 1 — Target of the deception (Kulchar)

| | 801 (in-litigation extrinsic fraud) | 802 (pre-litigation extrinsic fraud) |
|---|---|---|
| **Who was lied to** | Judge Wong, in MILs and on the record | The **insured claimant** (Rosario), in the Feb. 25, 2021 denial letter, in a **quasi-fiduciary** insurer–claimant posture |
| **Who was supposed to police it** | Cross-examination (failed — the lie was in MIL papers, not on the stand) | The claimant's own diligence (failed — by definition the claimant cannot test an investigation he was told had already "concluded") |
| **Result** | Judge denied continuance, granted MIL 17, excluded supplemental report, deferred 911 | Claimant did not know there was an evidentiary gap to preserve (911, CAD, BWC) before the **spoliation window** closed |

Both targets are outside the merits-fact-finder. That is the structural marker of extrinsic fraud under *Kulchar* (1969) 1 Cal.3d at 471. In 801 it is the bench; in 802 it is the pre-litigation claimant who cannot be told to "guard against it at trial" because there was no trial yet.

### Axis 2 — Caldwell "regularity vs. merits"

In 801, the fraud went to the regularity of the **judicial proceedings** (continuance, MIL rulings, exclusion gates). In 802, the fraud goes to the regularity of the **claims-handling process** — the statutory and common-law architecture that an insurer is obligated to run before issuing a denial:

- The February 25, 2021 denial letter said "investigation concluded" / "after careful review";
- 911, CAD, and BWC had not been obtained;
- The *form* of the communication was a categorical liability denial conveying investigative finality.

That is regularity-of-process fraud, not merits fraud. The merits of the underlying BI claim were never adjudicated in the claims-handling channel — that is the entire point. The fraud is in the **claim-process architecture,** not in the *content* of the bodily-injury determination.

This is why the SAC carefully separates the gravamen: the wrongful act is the **non-communicative failure to investigate** plus active concealment of that failure — not the denial letter's protected speech. That is the `Park v. Board of Trustees` / `City of Cotati v. Cashman` framing the 802 roadmap relies on, which neutralizes anti-SLAPP and litigation-privilege defenses without entering merits territory.

### Axis 3 — *Fraus illata curiae* / illegal-conduct exception

In 801 the deception lands directly on the bench, so `Action Apartment Assn., Inc. v. City of Santa Monica` (2007) 41 Cal.4th 1232 and the *Kulchar* extrinsic-fraud carve-out do the work. In 802 the deception lands on the claimant pre-suit, but the same two doctrines plus a third (`Flatley v. Mauro` (2006) 39 Cal.4th 299) carry it through the privilege gauntlet:

> *Action Apartment Assn., Inc. v. City of Santa Monica* (2007) 41 Cal.4th 1232 recognizes that even where the litigation privilege might otherwise apply, extrinsic fraud pierces that protection because it involves concealment that prevents a party from having their day in court. The concealment alleged here is extrinsic, not intrinsic. CSAA concealed its failure to investigate entirely in the pre-litigation period — before plaintiff knew there was an evidentiary gap to pursue, before counsel could have been directed to target specific records, and before any trial process gave plaintiff a forum to expose the concealment.

The mechanism is the same on both sides: deception **outside** the adversary contest that prevents the contest from being fair. In 801 it is outside the trial inside the case. In 802 it is outside the litigation entirely, before the case existed. Both are extrinsic.

### Axis 4 — "Prevented from presenting" — Kulchar's operative verb

| What plaintiff was prevented from doing | 801 | 802 |
|---|---|---|
| **Presenting at trial** | Continuance, MIL 17, supplemental report, 911 sequencing | n/a (different layer) |
| **Preserving evidence pre-suit** | n/a | 911 audio retention windows; CAD/BWC retention policies (~30/60-day deletion cycles); the moment CSAA falsely declared the investigation "concluded," plaintiff had no signal to issue spoliation letters or subpoenas to SFPD/dispatch |
| **Knowing the gap existed** | Concealed by "news to me" / "not produced in discovery" | Concealed by "facts currently available" / "investigation concluded" |
| **Calibrating litigation strategy** | Forced into trial unprepared | Forced into a posture where plaintiff *himself* had to procure the foundational records CSAA represented it had already collected (this is the *Prentice* damages anchor — out-of-pocket procurement, transcription, certification) |

802 SAC ¶ 22B specifically pleads non-curability, which is the second half of the *Kulchar* test (the *Pico v. Cohn* safety valve does not close because the concealment is structural and pre-litigation).

### Axis 5 — *Pico v. Cohn* / "should have been guarded against"

`Pico v. Cohn` (1891) 91 Cal. 129 says a fraud the diligent litigant could have caught at trial stays intrinsic. It fails in 802 for a different reason than in 801, but more decisively:

- **In 801** it fails because the truth (defense file contents) was held by the defense alone and the lie was in MIL papers and bench colloquies a *pro per* cannot impeach in real time.
- **In 802** it fails *a priori* because the deception was **complete before any litigation existed.** There was no trial in which to "guard against it." The claimant does not get cross-examination, document subpoenas, or motions in limine in the claims-adjustment process. The very pleading point in SAC ¶ 22B is that the fraud was **not curable through the litigation process itself** because by the time the case existed, the spoliation/preservation window for the pre-suit evidence had already closed.

*Pico*'s safety valve presupposes a forum in which the lie can be tested. 802's lie was told in a forum that has no such testing apparatus. Extrinsic by structural necessity.

## Part III — The Bridge: How 801's Extrinsic Fraud Loops Back Into 802 Via the Ratification / *Prentice* Chain

Here the two cases stop being parallel and become a single closed circuit. The 802 SAC does not need to plead the 801 trial-level fraud as a separate cause of action — it pleads it as **proximate consequential damages of the original 802 fraud,** attached to CSAA via authorization / ratification under Civil Code § 3294.

### 1. The *Prentice* hinge

`Prentice v. North American Title Guaranty Corp.` (1963) 59 Cal.2d 618 lets a fraud plaintiff recover attorney fees and litigation costs incurred in **separate proceedings** when those proceedings were the **direct, proximate, and foreseeable consequence** of the defendant's tort.

The 801 trial happened — including its corrupted MILs and 9-3 verdict — **because** of the original 2/25/2021 denial. CSAA's pre-litigation extrinsic fraud forced the underlying CGC-21-594102 litigation to exist at all, and forced plaintiff to litigate it without the foundational records CSAA had represented were in hand. The *Prentice* category in the 802 risk model is anchored at $1.125–1.875M (consequential 4-year litigation costs).

### 2. The ratification spine (Civ. Code § 3294(b); SAC ¶¶ 48B–C, 53)

> **¶ 48C.** Upon information and belief, Plaintiff alleges CSAA's officers, directors, or managing agents authorized, ratified, or were aware of and consciously disregarded these practices, thereby supporting punitive damages under Civil Code section 3294 to the extent proven.

CSAA paid for, directed, and ratified the PSA defense in the underlying case. PSA is the law firm whose attorneys (Navaratnasingham, Reyna) committed the 801 extrinsic fraud. Once 801 establishes that the trial verdict was procured by fraud-on-the-court committed by counsel **whom CSAA selected, funded, and supervised,** 802's punitive case writes itself: the carrier did not merely fail to investigate; it carried that failure forward into a litigation strategy that defrauded the trial court to defend the very denial that the failure to investigate produced.

### 3. The *Prentice* maturation

> From the bench, the realization is inescapable: the indemnifying carrier (CSAA) funded this corrupted architecture. The initial fraud (the "concluded" investigation denial in the underlying insurance claim) forced the litigation. The trial was won via an attorney's artifice. The Court will see *Prentice v. North American Title Guaranty Corp.* (1963) 59 Cal.2d 618 damages not as a speculative theory, but as the direct, proximate, and foreseeable consequence of the defense's unified strategy of suppression.

801's extrinsic-fraud finding is not just a vacatur predicate for the underlying tort verdict — it is also the **damages-amplification predicate** for 802. A *Prentice* finding does not require 802 to relitigate the trial-level fraud; it incorporates the 801 finding by reference and asks one question: was the corrupted CGC-21-594102 trial a proximate, foreseeable consequence of CSAA's 2/25/2021 fraud? Once 801 finds extrinsic fraud against CSAA-funded counsel, the answer is unavoidable.

## Part IV — The Integrated Picture (Bench Statement)

> 801 and 802 are the same fraud at two different layers, separated by time and forum but unified by the carrier. In 802, CSAA committed pre-litigation extrinsic fraud against its own claimant by representing that an investigation was "concluded" when the foundational records (911, CAD, BWC) had not been obtained — concealment that, by definition, was extrinsic because it preceded any forum in which it could have been tested (*Action Apartment Assn.*; SAC ¶¶ 22A–B). That concealment forced the underlying personal-injury litigation to exist. In 801, CSAA-selected and CSAA-funded counsel then committed in-litigation extrinsic fraud against the trial court by misrepresenting receipt and provenance of those same suppressed records — corrupting the procedural framework of the trial under *Kulchar* and *Caldwell*. The 802 ratification allegations (¶¶ 48B–C, 53) fold the 801 trial-level fraud back onto CSAA as authorized institutional conduct, and *Prentice* (1963) 59 Cal.2d 618 makes the costs of the corrupted underlying trial directly recoverable as 802 fraud damages. There is no doctrinal seam between the two cases — only a temporal one. Both layers are extrinsic by the same *Kulchar* / *Caldwell* / *Action Apartment* test, applied to the layer in which the lie was told.

## Part V — Why This Matters Strategically

1. **801 vacatur and 802 punitive exposure are mutually reinforcing.** A finding for plaintiff in 801 supplies the ratification amplifier in 802. A finding for plaintiff in 802 (especially on the institutional "deny-delay-defend" pattern in SAC ¶¶ 48B–C) supplies the institutional motive that explains why counsel committed the 801 fraud — i.e., not a one-off lawyer mistake, but the trial-level execution of the same statewide claims-handling pattern.
2. **The litigation-privilege / anti-SLAPP defense is foreclosed at both layers.** 801 pierces [Civ. Code § 47(b)](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/statutes-cal/CIV-47.md) via [*Rusheen v. Cohen* (2006) 37 Cal.4th 1048, 1065](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/opinions-pdf/Demurrer-cal-4th-vol37-p1048-Rusheen-v-Cohen.pdf) [Leg.Auth. #7](#leg-auth-7). 802 pierces it via [*Action Apartment Assn.*](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/opinions-pdf/Action-Apartment-Assn-v-City-of-Santa-Monica-41-Cal-4th-1232.pdf) [Leg.Auth. #5](#leg-auth-5) and [*Flatley v. Mauro*](CASE-LAW-AND-STATUTES/opinions-pdf/Demurrer-cal-4th-vol39-p299-Flatley-v-Mauro.pdf) [Leg.Auth. #6](#leg-auth-6). Same outcome, different doctrine, both extrinsic-fraud-driven.
3. **No harmless-error or no-causation defense on either side.** 801: structural fraud defies harmless-error analysis. 802: *Prentice* causation is satisfied as a matter of law once the underlying litigation is shown to be a foreseeable consequence of the original fraud.
4. **Pleading discipline in the 802 SAC is what protects the case from a *Moradi-Shalal* collapse.** By treating the denial letter as evidence of the non-investigation rather than as the wrongful act itself, the SAC keeps the gravamen on conduct (failure to investigate) and lets `Zhang v. Superior Court` (2009) 46 Cal.4th 1027 restore UCL standing for the *fraudulent*-prong unfair-competition claim. (See `DEFENSE/802/12-LEGAL-AUTHORITY-ROADMAP-AND-SAC-MAP.md` § E-6.)
