## Why these three artifacts together

The merits letter pairs Exhibit 1 against Exhibits 2 and 3 to identify a contradiction on the face of the record:

- **Exhibit 1 (Rash, 2021):** the investigation **was** performed.
- **Exhibits 2 and 3 (Priya, 2025):** the investigative product **was not** in counsel's possession.

Both cannot be true. The reconciliation, on the face of the record, *requires bearing false witness in one direction or the other*. The actionable wrong pleaded in CGC-25-631801 is the signing and submission of a sworn declaration containing material representations of fact contrary to the record then in counsel's actual or constructive possession; the actionable wrong pleaded in CGC-25-631802 is the externalization of the non-performance of the investigation through the issuance of a denial letter that represents the investigation as complete.
