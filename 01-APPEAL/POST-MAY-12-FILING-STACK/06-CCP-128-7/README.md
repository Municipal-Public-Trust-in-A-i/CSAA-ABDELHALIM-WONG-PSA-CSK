## Safe-Harbor Mechanics (CCP 128.7(c)(1))

Under CCP 128.7(c)(1), a motion for sanctions must:

1. Be served at least 21 days before filing with the court (safe harbor)
2. Not be filed if the challenged document, claim, or defense is withdrawn or corrected within the 21-day period
3. Describe the specific conduct alleged to violate subdivision (b)
