## Strategic Purpose

This sub-packet is a **hybrid record-preservation filing** with two components working together:

1. **Rule 3.3(a)(2) Notice** -- Plaintiff's continuing obligation to disclose *JKC3H8 v. Colton* (2013) 221 Cal.App.4th 468 to the tribunal; mirrors the 802 TAB-33A structure but reframed for the post-ruling, pre-NOE window.

2. **Statement Memorializing Omissions** -- A structured docket record identifying, by minute-order line number, the five bodies of controlling authority the May 12, 2026 anti-SLAPP order did not cite, apply, or distinguish. This is the core appellate-record document.

**Why file now (pre-NOE).** The 60-day appeal clock under California Rules of Court, rule 8.104(a) has not started because no Notice of Entry of the May 12 order has been served. Filing this packet now ensures that *JKC3H8*, the CCP 425.17(c) carve-out, the Insurance Code predicates, *Le Francois*, and the excluded-briefs issue all appear in the trial-court record before the clerk's transcript is compiled for the appeal. The Court expressly invited the appellate pathway at the May 12, 2026 hearing.
