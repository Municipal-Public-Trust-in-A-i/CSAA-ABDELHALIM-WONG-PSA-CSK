## Why this is filed under the appeal lane (not the 801 trial-court folder)

The May 12 hearing resolved the last major trial-court motion in CGC-25-631801. Post-hearing, the 801 case file is **appellate record** rather than an active trial-court docket. Materials filed after the Anti-SLAPP / special-motion ruling:

1. Preserve the record for A173827 (direct trial appeal).
2. Satisfy the exhaustion requirement for any future writ or collateral attack.
3. Comply with *Le Francois v. Goel* (2005) 35 Cal.4th 1094 (reconsideration motion as the mechanism to put new authority before the trial court before appellate briefing freezes the record).

The underlying 801 trial-court file remains at [02-CASE-CGC-25-631801/](../../02-CASE-CGC-25-631801/INDEX.md) — nothing there has been deleted or renamed. This folder adds the preservation layer on top.

## Key authorities governing the preservation posture

| Authority | Relevance |
|-----------|-----------|
| *Le Francois v. Goel* (2005) 35 Cal.4th 1094, 1107 | Court may reconsider its own motion on its own motion; CCP § 1008 motion tees up the issue for appellate review without waiver. |
| CCP § 1008(a) | Reconsideration motion; new facts, circumstances, or law. |
| Cal. Rules of Court, rule 8.120 | Appellate record must be self-contained; exhibits filed in the trial court become part of the record on appeal. |
| *Blank v. Kirwan* (1985) 39 Cal.3d 311 | Proposed pleading filed with leave-to-amend motion is preserved for appellate review of the denial. |
| Evidence Code §§ 452 to 453 | Judicial notice of governing law and official acts; each RJN lodges the authority into the trial-court file. |
