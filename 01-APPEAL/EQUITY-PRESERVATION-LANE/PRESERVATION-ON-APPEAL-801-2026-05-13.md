## 4. Evidence Code section 452: appropriate use for procedural facts

Use RFJN to **pin**:

- filed **MIL** captions and **orders** on MIL outcomes;
- **clerk’s transcript** pages referenced in minutes;
- **docket** entries showing **what** was filed and **when**.

Avoid briefing that implies RFJN **precludes** Anti-SLAPP or **binds** the court to treat MIL narratives as **fraud merits** findings. Align with **Evidence Code section 352** and your thematic `MASTER-REPORT-NOT-FULLY-ADJUDICATED` thesis where labeled as **plaintiff argument**.
