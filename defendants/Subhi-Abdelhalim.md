# Subhi Abdelhalim — driver / judgment beneficiary

**Roles and appearances:** Driver defendant and judgment beneficiary in the underlying trial (CGC-21-594102); named defendant in the independent equity action (CGC-25-631801).

## Stated position / claims
Plaintiff’s pleadings allege that Abdelhalim, through his counsel and in his own deposition, maintained that he was not negligent, did not flee the scene (no hit-and-run), and that the collision occurred under circumstances where the pedestrian was at fault.

## Rap sheet (conduct alleged in pleadings vs. record)

### Negligence and Hit-and-Run (Veh. Code 20002)
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege Abdelhalim negligently struck the pedestrian in the crosswalk and subsequently engaged in hit-and-run conduct by leaving the immediate scene without providing required information, contrary to Vehicle Code § 20002. This is actionable as the underlying tort and statutory violation that triggered the entire liability claim.

### Sworn Contradictions (alleged Penal Code 118 / perjury-grade conduct)
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege that Abdelhalim's sworn deposition testimony contradicted established documentary and audio evidence (e.g., 911 recordings, CAD dispatch times, and police body-worn camera statements) regarding his actions immediately after the collision. When sworn testimony materially contradicts fixed documentary evidence, it implicates perjury statutes and undermines the integrity of the fact-finding process.
