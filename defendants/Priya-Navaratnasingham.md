# Priya D. Navaratnasingham — defense counsel (PSA)

**Roles and appearances:** Trial attorney for Phillips, Spallas & Angstadt LLP in the underlying trial (CGC-21-594102); named defendant in the equity action (CGC-25-631801).

## Stated position / claims
Plaintiff’s pleadings allege that Navaratnasingham appeared at trial and pretrial hearings, arguing against the admission of plaintiff's 911 audio and police video on grounds of hearsay and lack of foundation. During Trial Day 7, she stated on the record that the defense had received recordings "provided by Dolan."

## Rap sheet (conduct alleged in pleadings vs. record)

### Extrinsic Fraud (The "Dolan" Admission)
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege that Navaratnasingham's admission on Trial Day 7—"Sure. They were provided by Dolan"—proves the defense secretly possessed and utilized unredacted or alternative audio/video evidence that had been concealed from the plaintiff and the court during discovery. This concealment is alleged to be the mechanism of extrinsic fraud that prevented a fair trial.

### Misrepresentation to Tribunal (Rules of Professional Conduct 3.3, 8.4(c), Bus. & Prof. Code 6068(d))
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege that by arguing to exclude evidence while secretly holding a "Dolan" copy, Navaratnasingham violated the duty of candor to the tribunal (RPC 3.3), engaged in misrepresentation (RPC 8.4(c)), and failed in the statutory duty not to mislead the judge (Bus. & Prof. Code § 6068(d)).
