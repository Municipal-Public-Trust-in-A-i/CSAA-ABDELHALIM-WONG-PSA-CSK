# Tyler J. O'Connell — counsel of record, non-party

**Roles and appearances:** Defense counsel for CSAA Insurance Exchange (Porter Scott) in the UCL action (CGC-25-631802). *Note: O'Connell is counsel of record and a non-party; he is listed here for a conduct-based rap sheet based on his filed declaration.*

## Stated position / claims
In his declaration filed in support of CSAA's anti-SLAPP motion, O'Connell summarizes the underlying trial and appeal, characterizing the plaintiff's UCL claims as arising from protected litigation activity (step one) and asserting procedural defaults and meritless claims (step two).

## Rap sheet (conduct alleged in pleadings vs. record)

### False Legal Narrative as Fact (Perjury / CCP 128.7 Implications)
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's anti-SLAPP rebuttal and evidentiary objections allege that O'Connell’s declaration improperly blends ultimate legal conclusions with purported facts (e.g., stating what the claims "arise from"). By signing a declaration under penalty of perjury that contains legal arguments disguised as factual personal knowledge, he allegedly violates the foundational requirements for declarations and the duty of candor (RPC 3.3), risking CCP § 128.7 sanctions.

### Misuse of Judicial Notice and Mischaracterization of Gravamen
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's rebuttal alleges O'Connell orchestrated a Request for Judicial Notice (RJN) that improperly attempts to establish the *truth* of matters asserted in court records, rather than merely their *existence* (violating Evid. Code 452/453). Furthermore, he allegedly mischaracterizes the gravamen of the UCL claim to fit the *Park* / *Baral* anti-SLAPP framework, misleading the tribunal (RPC 8.4(c), Bus. & Prof. Code 6068(d)).
