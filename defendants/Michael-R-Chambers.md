# Michael R. Chambers — original defense counsel

**Roles and appearances:** Original defense counsel of record (at Carbone, Smith & Koyama LLP); named defendant in the equity action (CGC-25-631801).

## Stated position / claims
Plaintiff’s pleadings allege that Chambers, while managing the defense file before its transfer, signed discovery responses and representations to the court claiming that certain police recordings and 911 audio were complete, inaccessible, or inadmissible, thereby framing the initial factual record.

## Rap sheet (conduct alleged in pleadings vs. record)

### Extrinsic Fraud and Duty of Candor (Rules of Professional Conduct 3.3, 3.4)
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege that Chambers failed to correct the record and suppressed the "Dolan" audio/video evidence during his tenure. By permitting incomplete or misleading discovery responses to stand without correction, he is alleged to have violated his duty of candor to the tribunal (RPC 3.3) and his duty of fairness to opposing counsel (RPC 3.4), contributing to the extrinsic fraud that tainted the trial preparation.
