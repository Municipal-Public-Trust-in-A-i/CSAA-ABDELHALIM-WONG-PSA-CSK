# Carbone, Smith & Koyama LLP — original defense firm

**Roles and appearances:** Original retained defense firm for Abdelhalim (funded by CSAA) in the underlying trial (CGC-21-594102); transferor of the defense file in May 2023; named defendant in the equity action (CGC-25-631801).

## Stated position / claims
Plaintiff’s pleadings allege that this firm, acting for the defense until May 2023, maintained that the 911 audio and police video evidence were either inadmissible or did not prove the plaintiff's case, while managing the initial discovery phases.

## Rap sheet (conduct alleged in pleadings vs. record)

### Extrinsic Fraud and Fraud on the Court (Rules of Professional Conduct 3.3, 3.4)
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege that Carbone, Smith & Koyama LLP initiated a pattern of evidence suppression and misdirection during the discovery phase. This includes failing to produce or accurately identify the provenance of the 911 audio and body-worn camera footage, which violated the duty of candor to the tribunal (RPC 3.3) and fairness to opposing party (RPC 3.4). The firm's transfer of the "poisoned" file to successor counsel continued this extrinsic fraud.
