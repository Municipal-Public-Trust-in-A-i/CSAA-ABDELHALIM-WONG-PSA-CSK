# Phillips, Spallas & Angstadt LLP — successor defense firm

**Roles and appearances:** Successor defense firm retained for the underlying trial (CGC-21-594102) after the May 2023 substitution; named defendant in the equity action (CGC-25-631801).

## Stated position / claims
Plaintiff’s pleadings allege that this firm received the "poisoned" defense file from prior counsel, adopted its defects, and continued the defense at trial. The firm's stated position during trial was to rely on the existing record, oppose the admission of late-discovered 911/police evidence, and defend the driver based on the established discovery posture.

## Rap sheet (conduct alleged in pleadings vs. record)

### Continuation/Ratification of Extrinsic Fraud
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege that Phillips, Spallas & Angstadt LLP ratified and continued the fraudulent scheme initiated by original counsel. Rather than correcting the record upon discovering the "Dolan" audio and other suppressed evidence, the firm allegedly used this hidden evidence to its advantage at trial while simultaneously objecting to plaintiff's attempts to introduce similar materials.

### Fraud on the Court (RPC 3.3, 8.4(c))
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege the firm directed its trial attorneys to make misleading statements during hearings and trial regarding the provenance and admissibility of key recordings. This conduct allegedly deceived the trial court into issuing exclusionary rulings, violating the duties of candor (RPC 3.3) and constituting dishonesty or misrepresentation (RPC 8.4(c)).
