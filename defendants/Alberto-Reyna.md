# Alberto Reyna — defense counsel (PSA)

**Roles and appearances:** Trial attorney for Phillips, Spallas & Angstadt LLP in the underlying trial (CGC-21-594102); named defendant in the equity action (CGC-25-631801).

## Stated position / claims
Plaintiff’s pleadings allege that Reyna served as co-counsel at trial, actively participating in evidentiary objections, witness examinations, and arguments designed to exclude the plaintiff's police and 911 audio evidence while relying on the "poisoned" defense record.

## Rap sheet (conduct alleged in pleadings vs. record)

### Fraud on the Court and Extrinsic Fraud (Aiding and Abetting)
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege that Reyna acted in concert with Navaratnasingham to present a false narrative to the trial court regarding the 911 and body-worn camera evidence. By adopting the firm's strategy to suppress the "Dolan" admission and leverage incomplete discovery, Reyna is alleged to have participated in the extrinsic fraud and violated his duties of candor (RPC 3.3).

### Misrepresentation to Tribunal (Bus. & Prof. Code 6068(d))
**Narrative:** Plaintiff's pleadings allege that Reyna failed to correct the record when false or misleading statements regarding evidence provenance were made in open court, violating the statutory duty to employ "those means only as are consistent with truth, and never to seek to mislead the judge or any judicial officer by an artifice or false statement of fact or law."
