# The Structural Contradiction at the Center of This Dispute
## "Completed Investigation" Versus "No Knowledge" — A Two-Page Summary

## 1. The Two Statements

Two statements have been made in this matter that cannot both be accurate at the same time. Both are documentary. Neither is in dispute as to its existence.

**Statement A — February 25, 2021 (CSAA denial letter).**
CSAA informed the claimant, in writing, that its investigation was **"concluded"** after **"careful review,"** and denied the claim.

**Statement B — January 2025 motions in limine and February 18, 2025 hearing (defense counsel, CGC-21-594102).**
Defense counsel represented that the 911 audio, body-worn camera footage, and unredacted materials were **"not produced,"** and characterized the materials as **"news to me."**

A third statement, made on April 16, 2025 (Trial Day 7), ties the first two together and collapses the defense's available positions:

**Statement C — April 16, 2025, certified trial transcript.**
> **MR. ROSARIO:** And the Dolan Law Firm provided the digital copies of the 911 calls, the body-worn camera footage, and all of the unredacted documents that —
>
> **MS. NAVARATNASINGHAM:** Sure. They were provided by Dolan. He has had them for whatever, sure.

Statement C confirms that the defense's own file chain — Dolan Law Firm to successor counsel to present counsel — held the materials throughout. That fact is not the plaintiff's characterization. It is on a certified reporter's transcript under the signatures of officers of the court.

## 2. Why the Two Statements Cannot Coexist

Statement A represents, to a claimant, that a regulated process was performed. Under Title 10, California Code of Regulations §§ 2695.4 and 2695.7, a "concluded" investigation means a "thorough, fair, objective" one. A thorough investigation of a pedestrian-versus-vehicle claim, starting from a police report that references a CAD number on its face, includes pulling the 911 audio, the body-worn camera, and the CAD record. Those are not optional or exotic steps; they are the obvious next steps.

Statement B represents that those same materials were unknown to the defense as of early 2025.

If Statement A is accurate, then the materials existed and were collected in 2021 — which contradicts Statement B's "news to me" framing in 2025. If Statement B is accurate, then the materials were not collected — which contradicts Statement A's representation of a completed investigation. Statement C (the Day-7 admission) eliminates any ambiguity: the materials were in the defense's own file chain from the earliest stages, which means Statement A was either accurate (materials collected but then omitted from the investigation's written conclusions) or false (investigation never completed), and Statement B cannot be accurate in either case.

## 3. The Legal Consequence of That Contradiction

The contradiction is not rhetorical. It drives elements in each of the three live cases. It does so in the same way regardless of which of the two statements the defense ultimately chooses to stand on.

**Appeal A173827 — prejudice (CCP § 614; Evidence Code § 451(a); *Soule*).** If the materials were available and known to the defense by trial, the concealment of the 911 audio and the refusal to instruct on Vehicle Code §§ 22107 and 21804(a) produced prejudice under a 9–3 liability verdict — the minimum civil threshold.

**CGC-25-631801 — extrinsic fraud (independent action in equity; *Kulchar* line; CCP § 473(d)).** Elements 1 (false representation) and 2 (prior possession / chain of custody) are independently proven by whichever of Statements A or B the defense disavows. Civil Code § 2338 (agency) and ratification principles carry findings to CSAA. Business & Professions Code § 6128(a) attaches personal attorney exposure.

**CGC-25-631802 — fraud, concealment, and UCL against CSAA.** If Statement A is stood on, the 2021 investigation was incomplete (because the materials were in the file chain but not used), making the "concluded" representation a material half-truth under Civil Code §§ 1709–1710 and Business & Professions Code § 17200. If Statement B is stood on, CSAA's retained counsel misrepresented the record in 2025, and CSAA — having funded, retained, and supervised that counsel — ratified the misrepresentation under Civil Code § 2338. Either path satisfies the "liability reasonably clear" trigger of Insurance Code § 790.03(h)(5).

There is no third option. Any attempt to reconcile Statements A and B without abandoning one requires impeaching the February 25, 2021 denial letter, the January 2025 motion papers, or the certified April 16, 2025 trial transcript. None of those documents can be withdrawn.

## 4. Why This Matters for Settlement Valuation

The contradiction is what converts a single claim-handling dispute into cross-case leverage. Because the same documentary record feeds all three matters, a ruling in any one forum — an interlocutory fraud finding in CGC-25-631801, a reversal and remand in A173827, a denial of summary judgment in CGC-25-631802 — creates findings that propagate. California judicial estoppel and issue preclusion operate on the defense's statements in each forum. Each week of continued litigation adds to the record without removing anything from it.

That dynamic is asymmetric. It raises the settlement floor over time; it does not lower it. The risk-surface models in this repository (per-case files in `RISK-SURFACE/per-case/`) place the worst-case CSAA-only exposure in the mid-nine-figure range at a 5:1 punitive ratio, with institutional second-order costs (DOI regulatory exposure, reinsurance pricing, UCL injunction compliance) above that.

## 5. The Narrow Question for the Defense

The defense is not being asked to concede that Statement A or Statement B is false. The defense is being asked to recognize that both statements exist on paper, in records maintained by neutral officers, and that the three live cases are structured so that whichever of those statements the defense eventually stands on will complete at least one element in at least one case. That recognition is the basis on which a negotiated resolution is materially less expensive than continued litigation.
