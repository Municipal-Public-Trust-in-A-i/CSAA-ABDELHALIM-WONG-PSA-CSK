## I. The Three-Case Landscape in One Picture

The defense is fighting a **three-front war with one shared spine of evidence**. Each forum has different parties, different remedies, and different burdens, but three factual nodes run through all three:

1. **The 911 audio**, which contains the insured Abdelhalim's spontaneous statement that he "just hit him … barely hitting anything."
2. **The body-worn-camera (BWC) footage and the CAD reference on the police report**, which law enforcement generated at the scene and which CSAA never pulled during its 2021 investigation.
3. **The governing Vehicle Code turning duties** — VC §§ 22107 and 21804(a), and the VC § 360 definition layer — which were simultaneously (a) pleaded in the operative complaint admitted as Defense Exhibit 500, (b) requested by the deliberating jury, (c) refused by the trial court on a "not in evidence" rationale, and (d) the mirror-image of what the police officer was allowed to opine on as "lay opinion."

A fourth fact sits on top of those three and binds them together procedurally: the **April 16, 2025 Trial Day 7 admission** by defense counsel Priya D. Navaratnasingham that the 911 calls, BWC footage, and unredacted documents "were provided by Dolan. He has had them for whatever, sure." That single on-the-record sentence collapses the chain-of-custody defense across 801, anchors the prejudice element of the appeal, and supplies the knowledge predicate that CSAA would otherwise need to deny in 802.

The three-case map:

| Matter | Forum | Defendants | Remedy Ceiling | Remedy Floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Appeal A173827** | 1st DCA | Abdelhalim only | Reversal + remand (no money) | Affirmance |
| **801 (CGC-25-631801)** | SF Superior | Abdelhalim, CSAA, PSA, Navaratnasingham, Reyna, Chambers, CSK | Vacatur + fraud finding + ancillary equitable | Dismissal |
| **802 (CGC-25-631802)** | SF Superior | CSAA only | ~$178.5M upper-bound (compensatory + 5:1 punitive + UCL) | Dismissal |

The appeal produces **no direct dollars** but creates the **retrial exposure** that reanimates PI damages against Abdelhalim (and derivatively, CSAA's indemnity). The 801 produces **equitable remedies and bar-referral shrapnel** against the attorney defendants but is "not the money case." The 802 is **the money case**, and it is the case where CSAA is alone on the caption with no co-defendants to share the column.

That asymmetry is the strategic center of gravity: the defense has **three chances to contain the record** (each with its own procedural gauntlet), but the *same factual admissions* feed all three. If the defense concedes any one node to win the case in front of it, the concession poisons the other two.

## II. Defense Logic Tree — Appeal A173827 (Abdelhalim as Respondent)

### A. What the defense must win on appeal

The defense has to defeat *every* prejudicial-error issue the appellant has preserved, because reversal on any one produces a remand under catastrophic evidentiary conditions. The appellant's issues (from the Opening Brief and per-case file) are:

1. CCP § 614 duty to inform on law — VC 22107/21804(a) were "law arising in the case";
2. Instructional error framing statutes as "not in evidence" when Evidence Code § 451(a) makes judicial notice mandatory;
3. Prejudice under *Soule* and *Watson* (9–3 verdict, moment-of-verdict error);
4. Asymmetric "not in evidence" treatment (VC § 360 supplied; VC §§ 22107/21804(a) refused);
5. Defense closing asymmetry (*Cassim*) — defense argued from Exh. 500 and officer fault opinion without reciprocal access for appellant.

### B. Decision nodes

```

│         objection was overruled on the record, and CCP 614 is ministerial

│
├── (3) Prejudice under Soule
│     ├── Argue error was harmless ─► 9–3 verdict is bare minimum; single juror
│     │   swing = different outcome
```

Each branch independently produces reversal if it lands. The defense must pay the **single-error penalty**: one prejudicial error wins the appeal. That is why the appeal is rated at 70–80% reversal probability in the per-case file.

### C. Why the appeal is the "free strike" that creates 801 and 802 leverage

Even though the appeal has a **$0 monetary ceiling**, it functions as the **detonator** for the other two cases:

- Reversal + remand = retrial under the full evidentiary record (911 audio authenticated, BWC footage admitted).
- At retrial, the party-admission ("barely hit") comes in under Evidence Code § 1220 and the 9–3 liability posture becomes a different case.
- That reopens the compensatory PI exposure that has been assumed away for four years — and that exposure is what CSAA's indemnity picks up, feeding into 801's CSAA-ratification count and 802's damages chain.

The defense's worst outcome is **appellate silence on the "not in evidence" error** — i.e., an affirmance on a narrow ground. Anything else creates a public appellate opinion that can be judicially noticed in both trial-court actions.

## III. Defense Logic Tree — 801 (Equity / Extrinsic Fraud)

The 801 SAC pleads seven causes of action. The **spine** is the independent action in equity to vacate the April 25, 2025 judgment for extrinsic fraud (with a CCP § 473(d) overlay). The other six counts (concealment, deceit, aiding-and-abetting/ratification, declaratory relief, attorney deceit under Bus. & Prof. Code § 6128(a), constructive fraud) orbit that spine and expand the list of liable actors.

### A. Four-element plaintiff burden → four mirror-image defense obstacles

Per the 801 logic diagrams, plaintiff must satisfy four elements for the equitable vacatur count:

| Element | Plaintiff proof | Defense obstacle |
|---|---|---|
| **E1. False Representation** | Navaratnasingham's "news to me" / MIL "not produced" statements | Must deny statements against certified transcripts |
| **E2. Chain of Custody / Prior Possession** | May 4, 2023 file transfer from CSK to PSA; Day-7 "provided by Dolan" admission | Must either repudiate Day-7 admission or admit 20-month possession |
| **E3. Scienter & Insurer Complicity** | CSAA funding/direction/ratification | Must convince court insurer was passive observer |
| **E4. Meritorious Case & Prejudice** | 911 "barely hit" admission + 9–3 verdict | Must argue concealed evidence was immaterial |

### B. The E2 trap — "provided by Dolan"

The Day-7 exhibit is the defense's structural problem. It is a **certified reporter's transcript**. Navaratnasingham said, on the record, that the 911 calls and BWC footage "were provided by Dolan. He has had them for whatever, sure." That single utterance does three things at once:

1. **Admits 18–20 months of possession** — which destroys the "news to me" framing used at the February 18, 2025 hearing and the "not produced" framing used in the January 17, 2025 MILs.
2. **Locks CSK into the chain** — because the Dolan-to-CSK-to-PSA handoff is the only way PSA could have received the audio, the defense either accepts CSK's production (exposing CSK) or denies receipt (forcing CSK to produce the transmittal index to defend itself).
3. **Forecloses "innocent mistake"** — *Kulchar*-style innocence depends on lack of opportunity to know the truth; the admission proves opportunity.

The logical result is what the 801 diagrams describe as a **closed trap**: whether the defendants admit or deny the RFAs on this point, plaintiff proves E1 and E2.

### C. E3 — the CSAA ratification chain

CSAA's posture in 801 is distinct. CSAA was **not** at counsel table; its liability runs through:

- **Civ. Code § 2338** (principal liable for wrongful acts of agent in scope of agency);
- **Aiding-and-abetting / conspiracy / ratification** (801 Count 4);
- **Funding and direction** of the Navaratnasingham/Reyna defense;
- **Benefit from the fraudulent verdict** (premium / reserve accounting).

The defense logic for CSAA in 801 is:

```
CSAA 801 DEFENSE TREE
│
├── (A) Deny knowledge of MIL content ─► must then produce claim notes / billing
│                                         records to prove "passive observer"
│   └── Production exposes ratification (what claims-examiner signed off on 
│       what PSA did)
│
├── (B) Assert privilege ─► plaintiff invokes crime-fraud exception using the 
│                           Day-7 admission as prima facie extrinsic fraud
│   └── Privilege log fight is winnable for plaintiff once E1 is proven 
│       (extrinsic fraud is the textbook crime-fraud trigger)
│
└── (C) Assert "independent counsel" ─► record shows CSAA hired PSA, paid PSA, 
                                        and retained control; 
                                        Civ. Code § 2338 liability vests
```

Every branch here **leaks material** into 802. CSAA cannot credibly deny in 801 facts it will need to deny in 802, and vice versa — the cases are colliding on the same factual substrate.

### D. The attorney-defendant tree (PSA, CSK, individual attorneys)

Navaratnasingham, Reyna, Chambers are separately exposed under **Bus. & Prof. Code § 6128(a)** (attorney deceit — treble damages + reasonable attorney's fees). This count has no good defense tree because the predicate acts are certified-transcript statements to the court. The per-case matrix puts PSA exposure at $3.75M–$6.25M and Chambers/CSK at $1.125M–$1.875M. The collateral consequences — State Bar referral, malpractice-carrier tender, permanent-record fraud finding — are "career-ending" severity per the per-case file.

### E. What the defense must concede to win 801

To defeat 801, the defense must either (i) convince the court the Day-7 admission is not what it says it is, or (ii) show that even if it is, the 911 audio was immaterial. Option (i) requires impeaching the court reporter. Option (ii) requires conceding that a defendant's spontaneous party-admission of fault is irrelevant to liability — which is a losing argument on its face and also **destroys the defense's appellate posture**, because the same "immaterial" argument fails under *Soule* prejudice analysis.

This is the recursive problem: the defense's 801 and appeal arguments are **logically incompatible**. In 801 the defense wants to say "the audio doesn't matter" (no prejudice from concealment). On appeal the defense needs to say "the jury didn't need the VC statutes" (no prejudice from instructional error). In both, the defense is asserting that the jury's 9–3 finding was not marginal. But the verdict *was* 9–3, and saying so in one forum is admissible in the other.

The **bottleneck** is the claims manual. Once the internal claims manual is produced in response to RFP 1–4 (after SROG 6–12 forces the narrative), the court and jury can compare CSAA's written standard of care against its 2021 conduct. The 802 logic diagrams treat this as the pivot point: **Element 4** (bad faith / liability reasonably clear) resolves either through direct admission of the 911 audio's content *or* through compelled production of internal policies showing systemic failure to follow the policy. Either path closes the element.

## V. Cross-Case Overlap Matrix — The Shared Spine

This section is the heart of the request. Five overlap nodes run through all three matters; each one compresses the defense's options across forums.

### Overlap Node 1 — The 911 Audio ("barely hit")

| Case | Function of 911 in the case | What the defense needs to say | Contradiction generated |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Appeal** | Prejudice under *Soule*: concealed party-admission in a 9–3 verdict | "Not material / harmless" | Cannot reconcile with 802 damages argument that the audio was not part of the 2021 record |
| **801** | E4 — meritorious case and prejudice from fraudulent concealment | "Not material / duplicative" | Same as above; also contradicts Day-7 admission's implication that the audio was significant enough to be addressed mid-trial |
| **802** | E4 — bad faith because liability was reasonably clear if the audio had been pulled | "Audio was not required / irrelevant" | Collapses industry-custom expert testimony; contradicts the police report's CAD reference |

The 911 is a **party-admission under Evidence Code § 1220**. Once authenticated (Abdelhalim already authenticated his voice at trial — "Yes. That is my voice."), the audio is admissible everywhere. That means the defense cannot win by keeping it out; it can only win by arguing it is not important. But arguing the admission of a party to having "just hit" the plaintiff is not important is not a winning argument in front of any fact-finder, and it is structurally incompatible with the arguments the defense needs to run on appeal and in 801.

### Overlap Node 2 — The Body-Worn Camera and CAD Number

| Case | Function of BWC/CAD | Defense position | Contradiction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Appeal** | Supports prejudice; BWC would have corroborated the crosswalk/turning geometry and undercut officer's "lay opinion" fault | "Not preserved / not in evidence" | Same "not in evidence" framing that produces reversible error on VC statutes |
| **801** | E2 — file transfer contained BWC; Day-7 admission names "body-worn camera footage" | "Not received / don't know" | Day-7 transcript |
| **802** | E2 — CSAA had the police report with CAD reference; never pulled BWC | "Not standard / not required" | Title 10 CCR § 2695.7; industry custom |

The BWC is the **document-intensive** overlap. Unlike the 911, which is one audio file, the BWC is a multi-file evidentiary trail. It implicates:

- **Dolan Law Firm's** prior-counsel production index (external custodian);
- **CSK's** transmittal log (internal defense-file custodian);
- **PSA's** intake log (current defense-file custodian);
- **SFPD's** CAD system (governmental third party).

Each of those custodians generates its own paper trail. When the defense tries to deny receipt or possession, those four trails have to all line up with the denial. They do not. The repository already documents that Dolan "provided" them (per Navaratnasingham's Day-7 statement), which means the chain is closed.

### Overlap Node 3 — Vehicle Code §§ 22107, 21804(a), and 360

This is the appeal's core — and it bleeds into both 801 and 802.

| Case | Function of VC 22107/21804 | Defense position | Contradiction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Appeal** | Jury asked for it by number; court refused on "not in evidence" rationale; CACI 707 was requested pretrial | Affirm the trial court's discretion | 9–3 verdict + *Soule* + Evidence Code § 451(a) |
| **801** | Supports prejudice (E4) — merits value of the concealed evidence depends on the governing duty of care | Irrelevant to equity | False; *Kulchar* meritorious-case prong requires showing the concealed evidence would have produced different outcome *under applicable law*; the applicable law is VC 22107 |
| **802** | "Liability reasonably clear" (Ins. Code § 790.03(h)(5)) depends on applying the 911 admission to the duty of care | No duty to apply statutes pre-litigation | Claims examiners are expected to know VC turning duties; police reports reference them; *Hangarter* and *Major* framework applies |

The VC 22107 node is interesting because it is **procedurally asymmetric** across the three forums. On appeal, it is the direct subject-matter of the reversible error. In 801, it is the *legal yardstick* against which the meritoriousness of plaintiff's case is measured (you cannot show the fraud was prejudicial without showing what the correct duty was). In 802, it is the *standard of care* that an insurance adjuster should have applied to the police report + 911 audio to conclude that liability was reasonably clear under § 790.03(h)(5).

The defense cannot disown VC 22107 in one forum without handing the plaintiff a tool in the other two. **Defense Exhibit 500** — the original complaint — was admitted at trial and argued from in closing; it pleads VC §§ 22107 and 21804(a). The record is already inconsistent with any "these statutes were not in the case" posture.

### Overlap Node 4 — The April 16, 2025 "Provided by Dolan" Admission

This is the single most damaging record fact to the defense. It is a **certified transcript statement** by Navaratnasingham, as counsel of record, on Trial Day 7:

> **MR. ROSARIO:** And the Dolan Law Firm provided the digital copies of the 911 calls, the body-worn camera footage, and all of the unredacted documents that —
>
> **MS. NAVARATNASINGHAM:** Sure. They were provided by Dolan. He has had them for whatever, sure.

This sentence has four structural effects across the three cases:

1. **Appeal — prejudice amplifier.** Confirms the materials were in the defense's hands long before trial, which means the jury-instruction errors occurred in a record where the defense already possessed the very evidence the jury needed to resolve the liability question.
2. **801 — E1 and E2 simultaneous proof.** It flatly contradicts the February 18, 2025 "news to me" statement (E1 — false representation) and establishes prior possession (E2 — chain of custody).
3. **801 — E3 amplifier.** CSAA-funded counsel cannot have made both statements without CSAA's constructive knowledge or reckless failure to monitor — either state of mind supports ratification.
4. **802 — temporal destruction of "concluded" investigation.** If Dolan had the materials and provided them to successive counsel, the materials existed and were accessible by 2021; CSAA's choice not to acquire them is a deliberate decision, not a limitation of available evidence.

### Overlap Node 5 — CSAA's Funding, Control, and Ratification

CSAA paid for the defense in the underlying 594102 action. That single fact — which CSAA cannot deny, because billing records and defense-counsel retention letters exist — is the load-bearing beam across 801 and 802:

| Case | Effect of CSAA funding |

The per-case files explicitly note this: 801 is "strengthened by" the appeal's reversal narrative, and 802 is "strengthened by" the 801 fraud finding.

## VI. Implications for the Defense Based on the Actual Record

Given the overlaps, the defense's **global** implications are:

### 1. Conceding any one node poisons the others

- Conceding the 911 audio is material in the appeal destroys the 801 "immateriality" argument.
- Conceding CSAA did not direct the defense in 801 contradicts CSAA's ability to run an "independent counsel" defense in 802, because in 802 CSAA has already invoked its own selection and retention authority over PSA.
- Conceding in 802 that the 911/BWC were not "available" in 2021 contradicts Day-7's admission that Dolan had them from the start.

### 2. Running inconsistent theories across cases is itself a source of liability

California recognizes **judicial estoppel**. A party who successfully maintains position X in one proceeding cannot later take position not-X in another. The defense here is structurally prone to inconsistency because its best argument in each forum contradicts its best argument in another forum. The plaintiff's per-case files flag this explicitly (issue preclusion / judicial estoppel anchors).

### 3. Discovery in 801 and 802 will generate a public record before the appeal is decided

Even if the appeal is affirmed (low probability per the per-case file's 70–80% reversal rating), the 801 and 802 discovery machinery will produce claim-file metadata, billing, and transmittal logs that establish (i) CSAA's knowledge and (ii) the provenance chain. That public record is independently lethal to CSAA's institutional posture — it exists regardless of appellate outcome.

### 4. Attorney defendants face uncontainable collateral exposure

PSA, CSK, Chambers, Navaratnasingham, Reyna have **no insurance-funded buffer** for the State Bar and Business & Professions Code § 6128(a) liability. Their exposure is personal. The risk-surface per-case file puts PSA subtotal at $3.75M–$6.25M; Chambers/CSK at $1.125M–$1.875M; with career-ending collateral severity. Once the 801 court makes a fraud finding, these defendants may have independent incentives to cooperate — including testifying to CSAA's role in a way CSAA cannot control.

### 5. The Anti-SLAPP / demurrer posture is a bleeding trap

The risk-surface litigation memo makes the point that anti-SLAPP loss produces **mandatory fee-shifting** to the plaintiff and **merits preview** from the court. Running anti-SLAPP and demurrer simultaneously in 801 and 802 multiplies fee-motion exposure and multiplies the adverse merits preview footprint. The defense's threshold posture is **procedurally maximalist in a record that does not reward it**.

### 6. The retrial geometry has changed under the defense's feet

Even before appellate decision, the defense is now defending a universe in which:

- The 911 audio is authenticated and judicially noted.
- The BWC is in plaintiff's possession (via Dolan production).
- The Day-7 admission is certified.
- The Vehicle Code duty framework has been *litigated* (even if the trial court got it wrong) and will appear in any retrial jury charge.

Retrial is no longer a blank slate. It is a **hostile re-trial**.

## VII. Worst-Case Scenario for CSAA

CSAA is the deep pocket and the only defendant who is on all three maps (appellate indemnity, 801 co-defendant, 802 sole defendant). The worst-case scenario is not one bad ruling — it is the **cascade scenario** where each forum resolves against CSAA in sequence and the rulings compound.

### Stage 1 — Appeal A173827 reverses (Year 1, Q2–Q4)

- 1st DCA reverses on instructional error + prejudice.
- Remand for new trial against Abdelhalim.
- **Direct CSAA consequence:** indemnity obligation reactivates for retrial. Defense cost meter restarts. Reserve accounting has to be restated. Abdelhalim's retrial exposure (per the per-case model's retrial-CGC-21-594102 file) lands on CSAA policy limits first and then on CSAA for bad-faith failure-to-settle if the retrial verdict exceeds the limits.

### Stage 2 — 801 demurrer overruled, anti-SLAPP denied, fraud finding proceeds (Year 1, Q3 – Year 2, Q2)

- Court overrules demurrer (pleading survives in materially current form).
- Court denies anti-SLAPP; **mandatory attorney-fee shifting** under CCP § 425.16(c).
- Discovery produces claim file, billing records, and communications between PSA/CSAA.
- Court makes an interlocutory fraud finding or denies MSJ on fraud counts.
- **Direct CSAA consequence (801 only):** ancillary equitable exposure $375K–$625K (direct); contribution/indemnity from co-defendants $750K–$1.25M (that money flows *through* CSAA, not just to it, if co-defendants are insolvent or partially covered); plus crime-fraud privilege pierce allowing plaintiff to read into CSAA's internal litigation strategy.

### Stage 3 — 801 fraud finding becomes 802 issue-preclusion anchor (Year 2, Q2–Q4)

- The 801 fraud finding — especially a finding that CSAA ratified PSA's false representations — is brought into 802 via judicial notice and issue preclusion.
- CSAA's "passive-insurer" defense in 802 collapses.
- CSAA now defends 802 with **one hand tied** — the ratification element is effectively proven.

### Stage 4 — 802 verdict (Year 2, Q4 – Year 3)

- Jury finds for plaintiff on all three counts (fraud, concealment, UCL).

## VIII. Synthesis

The three cases are not three cases. They are **one evidentiary spine expressed in three procedural forums**. The 911 audio, the BWC footage, the VC turning-duty statutes, the April 16, 2025 "provided by Dolan" admission, and the CSAA funding-and-ratification chain form a shared factual substrate that each forum examines through a different legal lens.

Running privilege in 801 contradicts ratification-denial in 802. Arguing "not in evidence" on appeal has already been documented on a record that included the very statutes in Exhibit 500. Every available defensive move in one forum **creates** a plaintiff tool in another.

For CSAA specifically, the worst-case scenario is not a single large verdict. CSAA's only rational off-ramp is a **global resolution before the 801 interlocutory fraud finding and before the appellate decision**, and that window is closing.
