## Capsule summary

On **May 12, 2026**, Dept. 301 heard defendants' **anti-SLAPP special motion to strike** plaintiff's **First Amended Complaint ("FAC")**. The entered order (filed **Aug 6, 2026**) restates the court's **two-step § 425.16** analysis, finds **prong one** satisfied (protected litigation activity), and **prong two** not satisfied (no probability of prevailing). The court treats plaintiff's theory as **intrinsic fraud**, not cognizable **extrinsic fraud** vacatur, discusses **Civil Code § 47(b)** and **ancillary monetary relief**, **grants** the motion **in its entirety**, reserves **fees** for separate motion, excludes **unauthorized supplemental opposition** briefs, and **considers** plaintiff's **requests for judicial notice**. Closing line: **"IT IS SO ORDERED."**
