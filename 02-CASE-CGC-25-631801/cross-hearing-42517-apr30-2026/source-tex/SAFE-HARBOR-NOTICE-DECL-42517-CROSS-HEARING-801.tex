\section*{I. SAFE HARBOR NOTICE UNDER SECTION 128.7, SUBDIVISION (c)(1)}

\textbf{IMPORTANT: SERVICE ONLY.} Plaintiff Franciscus Dylan Rosario submits this Notice and Declaration pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 128.7 only for \textbf{service} on offending parties and their counsel. This Notice is \textbf{not a filing} for clerk registration for sanctions purposes unless and until any motion that may follow complies with subdivisions (c)(1) (twenty-one calendar day harbor) and (j) (service), and Plaintiff elects to file such a motion thereafter.

\textbf{Notice to offending parties and counsel.} Plaintiff gives notice pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 128.7, subdivision (c)(1), concerning papers and contentions Plaintiff contends violate section 128.7, subdivision (b), including subdivisions \textbf{(2)} and \textbf{(3)} and grounds involving insufficiently supported \textbf{factual contentions}, as particularized in the Exhibit Key and underlying memoranda served concurrently. This Notice is conditioned on Plaintiff's contemporaneous service of the explanatory memoranda and lodged authorities listed in the Exhibit Key below, which Plaintiff expects to cite as portions of supporting papers accompanying any contemplated motion for sanctions (section 128.7, subd.\ (j)).

\textbf{Twenty-one calendar-day safe harbor.} Under subdivision (c)(1), a motion for sanctions under section 128.7 ordinarily \textit{must not be filed with nor presented to the court} unless, after \textbf{service} upon the offending party of \textit{the motion and any supporting papers}, the challenged papers or positions are \textbf{not withdrawn or appropriately corrected within twenty-one calendar days}. By this Notice Plaintiff triggers that harbor concerning the challenged matters described herein. Plaintiff \textbf{reserves all rights}; nothing herein waives any motion, objection, citation to authority, sanctions request in other vehicles, regulatory complaint, fee motion, opposition, amendment, judicial notice objection, remedy, appeal, extraordinary writ petition, tolling or discovery argument expressly or impliedly waived.

\textbf{Challenged matters (within the meaning of subdivisions (f) through (j)).} In this Court (Dept.\ 301), the challenged proceedings and papers Plaintiff identifies for safe-harbor purposes include, without implied limitation, defendants' operative \textbf{special motion(s) to strike} under Code of Civil Procedure section 425.16; \textbf{motion(s) under section 436} attacking the pleadings where those papers import anti-SLAPP or ``absolute immunity'' themes inappropriate to statutory strike practice; related \textbf{demurrers} relying on defenses or theories incorporated from such motions and related papers; defenses and oppositions in the \textbf{MFL/SAC} sequence on calendar Apr.\ 30, 2026 that repackage the identical ``protected activity'' characterization; accompanying \textbf{oppositions/replies}; and affirmative defenses asserting availability of immunity from Plaintiff's statutes-based claims inconsistent with Plaintiff's lodged black-letter exhibits. Plaintiff incorporates by reference the caption cross-notice calendar for Dept.\ 301.
