## Relief requested

- Strike the **entire** First Amended Complaint (Feb. 25, 2026), **or in the alternative** strike all claimed **damages** (attorney’s fees, monetary damages, and other items) under C.C.P. §§ 435/436.

## Main FAC / prayer targets (categories listed in notice)

The notice itemizes multiple buckets tied to **FAC page/line** references, including in substance:

1. **Litigation costs and economic injury** (e.g. FAC pg. 20 line 7 et seq.): transcripts, copies, filing/service, travel, etc.  
2. **Lost earnings / lost productive time** (FAC pg. 20 lines 12–17).  
3. **Additional medical, therapy, pharmacy** (FAC pg. 20 lines 18–25).  
4. **Ancillary equitable monetary relief** and related (FAC pg. 20 lines 2–6; pg. 23 lines 26–28).  
5. **Prayer for relief** including vacatur, new trial, equitable monetary relief, costs (FAC pg. 27 lines 10–26).

The notice also includes **argument** that the FAC is inconsistent regarding attorney’s fees, and references significant **fee/costs exposure** from the prior action.
