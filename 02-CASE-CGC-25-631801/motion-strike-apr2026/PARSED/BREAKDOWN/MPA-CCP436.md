## Relief and theory (summary)

- Seeks to strike the **FAC in full** or **damages** as **irrelevant, false, improper, or nonconforming** under **C.C.P. § 436**, after establishing **legal standard** under **C.C.P. §§ 435/436** (*Coyne* / Code text).  
- **Extrinsic-fraud / equitable** theory: argues plaintiff cannot state **Kulchar**-type **extrinsic fraud** where plaintiff participated in the underlying trial; **appeal** (A173827) is the proper channel.  
- **Civ. Code § 47(b)**: **litigation privilege** / *Ribas* bar to damage theories flowing from **judicial proceeding** conduct.  
- **Standing** (*Shaolian*, *Norton*, *Omega*): no direct duty / no assignment.  
- **Collateral attack / estoppel** (*F.E.V.*, *Rico*/*Wouldridge*, *Singh*, *U.S. v. Throckmorton*): cannot relitigate; **9–3 defense verdict** and **costs**.  
- **C.C.P. § 1032** “prevailing party” / costs: plaintiff did not prevail below.

## Counter-theme hooks (for your opposition record)

- **CCP § 436** is **discretionary**; “**irrelevant, false, or improper**” must match **FAC** allegations as pleaded; fraud-on-the-court and **extrinsic** theories may present **factual** issues not suitable for **facial** strike.  
- **Civ. Code § 47(b)** **absolute privilege** does not necessarily defeat **independent** theories framed **outside** protected conduct or **fraud** on the tribunal (record-specific).  
- **Kulchar** / **estoppel** defenses turn on **what** was “fully litigated” and whether 802/801 **targets** the same “issue” or **fraud** predicates.
