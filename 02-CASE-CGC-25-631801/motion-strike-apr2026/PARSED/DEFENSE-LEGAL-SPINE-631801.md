## 2. Legal standard (defense)

- **C.C.P. §§ 435–436** — *Coyne* 35 Cal.2d 257; court may strike **irrelevant, false, or improper** matter.  
- **Civ. Code § 47(b)** — **absolute** **litigation** **privilege**; *Ribas* 38 Cal.3d 355 re IIED / “clear bar” when the FAC targets **protected** **judicial** **proceeding** **conduct**.  
- **Evid. Code** **452/453** + **C.C.P. § 430.70** — **RFJN** to **operative** **FAC** and **594102** **docket** / **judgment** (Ex. A–C).  
- **“Extrinsic fraud” / equity** — *Kulchar* 1 Cal.3d 467 (denial of **fair** **hearing** / “real contest”); movants argue **plaintiff** had **full** **participation** and **appeal** (A173827) is the **avenue** — not this **collateral** **suit**.  
- **Standing** — *Shaolian* 71 Cal.App.4th 268; *Norton* 49 Cal.App.3d 917; *Omega* 146 Cal.App.3d 470.  
- **Collateral attack / estoppel** — *F.E.V.* 15 Cal.App.5th 463, *Rico* 58 Cal.App.2d 878, *Wouldridge* 265 Cal.App.2d 82, *Singh* 227 Cal.App.4th 813, *Throckmorton* 98 U.S. 61.  
- **C.C.P. § 1032** / **“prevailing party”** — costs **cannot** be **recast** as **ancillary** **“equitable”** **monetary** **relief** (defense **frame**).
