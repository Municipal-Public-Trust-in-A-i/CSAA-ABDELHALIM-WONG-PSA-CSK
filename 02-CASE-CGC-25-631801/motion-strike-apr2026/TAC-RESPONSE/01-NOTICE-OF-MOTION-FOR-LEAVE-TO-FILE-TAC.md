## GROUNDS FOR MOTION

This Motion is made on the following grounds:

### 1. Liberal Amendment Policy

California courts have a strong policy favoring resolution of disputes on their merits. Code of Civil Procedure § 473 grants courts broad discretion to allow amendments to pleadings, and that discretion should be exercised liberally in favor of amendments. *Nestle v. City of Santa Monica* (1972) 6 Cal.3d 920, 939.

### 2. Response to Defense CCP § 436 Motion — Specific Cure of Eight Objections

The proposed Third Amended Complaint specifically cures the eight objections raised in Defendants' April 22, 2026 Motion to Strike under CCP § 436:

| Defense Objection | TAC Cure Section |
|-------------------|------------------|
| (1) *Kulchar* extrinsic-fraud viability | Section I: Four-channel framework with judicial-reliance quote |
| (2) Appeal-is-the-remedy | Section IV.5: *Kougasian/Stevenot* independent action doctrine |
| (3) Civil Code § 47(b) privilege | Section II: Nine-Point Chain; *Flatley/Action Apartment* illegality anchor |
| (4) Standing / *Shaolian-Norton* | Section III: *Estate of Sanders*; *Doctors' Company* framework |
| (5) Collateral estoppel | Section IV: Five-issues-not-adjudicated; primary-rights table |
| (6) CCP § 1032 prevailing-party | Section V: Express disclaimer |
| (7) Damages items / duplicative | Section V: Reservation paragraph; fraud-delta limitation |
| (8) IIED / *Ribas* (not asserted) | Section VI, Tier 4: Emotional distress as equity factor only |

### 3. No Prejudice to Defendants

Defendants will not be prejudiced by the proposed amendments:

- The TAC does not add any new parties beyond those already named in the operative complaint.
- The TAC does not assert fundamentally new theories of liability—extrinsic fraud, constructive fraud, and § 6128(a) violations were already alleged. The TAC pleads these theories with greater specificity.
- No additional discovery should be necessary as a result of the amendments.
- The case is at an early stage.

### 4. Good Faith Amendment

The proposed amendments are made in good faith to address objections raised in Defendants' CCP § 436 Motion to Strike. Plaintiff has diligently responded to the motion by preparing curative amendments.

### 5. Meritorious Claims Supported by Certified Record

The proposed Third Amended Complaint states meritorious claims supported by the certified record, including:

- The February 28, 2025 judicial-reliance quote (Hr'g Tr. pp. 291:26–292:7);
- The April 16, 2025 "provided by Dolan" admission (RT 7, p. 213, ll. 13–15);
- The nine-point evidentiary chain documenting 893 days of defense possession prior to categorical denial.
